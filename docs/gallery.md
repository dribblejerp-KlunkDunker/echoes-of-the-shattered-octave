## Release road R5b: the Moot-house, the Bell Yard and Ashlane

Two more areas in Whisper-Grove, both reached from places you can already get to.
- **The Moot-house** gains a stair up to its **gallery**, where Sable first saw a proctor watching the plaza for an hour, and to the muniment store where she keeps her desk: the sum of twenty names a night, and a practice sheet with one word written over and over. A stair goes down to the **cellar**, where Corwin Aye's barrel "has not leaked in nineteen years".
- **The Bell Yard and Ashlane,** through an archway off the Sunken Court: swept streets, latched shutters, a window-box of dead flowers, lamps with no oil, carts with their shafts up, Hollows keeping their small tasks, and proctors walking it. Halder Wick's house has a table laid for four. Ivo Renn's has two bowls set out for supper.
- **"The Bell Yard Roll":** seven doors carry name-boards. Write each household into your ledger.
- **"Do not comfort yourself in the margin":** in Ashlane a man sits against a wall doing nothing at all, pressed a second time. Tell Sable, and she shows you his entry and the Veil's three rules.
- Both quests close with a logbook line when the Kestrel leaves Whisper-Grove.

## Release road R5a: the Hollow and the Carders' house

Two new areas inside Oakhaven, each joined to a map you can already reach.
- **The Hollow,** through the tannery's back door: the lanes where the town keeps its burl-sick, 31 households behind barred doors, all humming. Doors past cutting carry the sap-mark, a green circle with a line struck through it. Lissa Merrow, eleven, sits on the step of the red-shutter house with her own tin cup. On Rootday mornings the Grafters' wagon comes in on felt-wrapped wheels, with a brazier, three masked Grafters and a Legionnaire.
- **"The List":** Rooke's ward-book is chained at the well-head, and the Inquisitor wants it. Two doors are pencilled, not chalked. Hum the tending note through each (or leave honey), then write "Recheck next month" against them.
- **The Carders' house,** behind the blue door on the north row: Pim's pallet pulled away from the wall, a hush-lamp in the window, and Lyra's chalk note. Its root-cellar is a short lesson in the Hush and the hush-mites.
- **"Keep him out of the damp":** once Pim is safe, Nell asks for the cellar to be cleared and a hot brick put in his pallet.
- At the Greening the Hollow goes under bramble and the blue door is barred again; quests left open close with a logbook line.

## Release road R4: the thin places, filled

Every place that was thin now has people, something to use and a reason to be there.
- **Oakhaven:** the baker's and the chandler's open. Agnes Tull, the baker's wife, punches the dough down every hour of the night and sells bread; Ambrose Vettle sells hush-whistles and bandages. Bettany, Hob and Old Tam keep a day place and a night place.
- **Small Oakhaven life:** the iron-oaks chime when the wind is up; a sapling comes up through the cobbles on Cooper's Row every morning with a hand-saw beside it; the sexton salts the chapel paths at dawn; the tanker-wagon comes through once a day between two Legionnaires; dogs howl at noon.
- **The Watch Downs** (still danger 0): Abel Sedge cuts turf by the fold and says the sign's "There are no sheep" out loud; the dew-pond gives a drink once a day.
- **Wethercombe:** the farmhouse (Mother Hurst at the hearth), the barn (Jem Cotter the shearer, barred in against the woolwight) and a shut-up cottage open; the Bellwether rents a room and keeps a slate of tabs.
- **The Flume Road:** Ennis Crowle keeps her skeps; what she says follows Bettany's Telling the Bees.
- **The tannery:** two hands at the vats, and the back door onto the Hollow (open since R5a).
- **The Low Rings and Stillwater Landing:** a punt-poler and a salvager in the Rings; a smuggler and a broadsheet-seller on the quay, with a board of Cantabile broadsheets.

## Release road R3: the Narrow comes home

Book One's rule, *too loud brings the hounds, too still brings the Hush*, now runs in Oakhaven and the systems that were already there.
- **Hush-lamps** hold the Hush off: a lit lamp stops the stillness timer underground, and a hush-whistle blown outdoors or underground is planted on its stake and pipes on as a lamp for a minute.
- **The Narrow is law inside the Hedge.** Make a loud noise where someone can see you and you get the ward-sign. Do it again soon after and the watch fines you for a breach of the Narrow.
- **Songs are learned from people,** through Hold the Pitch: Lyra teaches the Root note, Bettany the tending breath (for coin), and Old Tam "The Miller's Daughter" once you have told him the truth about the Flume. Page two of Tobin's notebook teaches the falling fifth at his reed-pipes. "The Miller's Daughter" is a new Song: loud, and every hound in hearing goes to where you sang it.
- **Hounds hunt in packs** behind a larger leader. A falling fifth that stops the leader, yours or Lyra's, routs the pack.
- **Lyra before her voice returns** whistles the fifth, and she can aim it at the one you are looking at; her heal is the smaller tending breath.
- **Three canon combat rules:** below 30% health your breath is loud; a Quiet goes to a lit lantern; where a hound bursts, nothing carries for a while.
- **The Listening Hour.** At dusk a lamplighter lights Oakhaven's hush-lamps one by one (they are dark by day), night-hummers keep three crossings till dawn, and at the dead hour the shift changes with "all's well, all's quiet, keep to the Narrow".
- **Standing (the Concordance)** is fed by choices you already make: Tam, Pim, Sable, the bottles and the heists. It moves shop prices, a line from Harrow and from Hetty, and one line of the ending. No numbers on screen.

|---|---|

Peer-to-peer over WebRTC with PeerJS's free matchmaker (and its free relay for mobile data), or codes swapped by hand with no matchmaker at all. No game server of our own, no accounts.
