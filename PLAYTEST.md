# Playtest checklist

This file lists what the automated tests cannot judge: feel, timing, how busy or quiet a place is, and whether a scene reads well. It is updated as each milestone lands. Newest items are at the top. Tick a box when you have looked, and write what you found under **Notes** in each section. Claude reads those notes and turns them into fixes or gate notes.

**Getting around quickly**
- **Rest** (the REST button) moves the clock 4 or 8 hours. Use it to reach dusk, night or noon.
- **The test console** opens with Shift + ` (backquote). Its codes are `richman` (money), `octave` (every Song), `ghostmode` and `showme` (reveals the map). Using any code marks the save.
- **Reaching each area:**
  - Oakhaven's interiors are behind their doors.
  - The Watch Downs are west of the clearing.
  - Wethercombe is past the Downs stile.
  - The Flume Road runs from Oakhaven's east Hedge door.
  - The Hollow is through the tannery's back door; the Carders' house is the blue door halfway down its lane.
  - Lyra's stone is behind the old cooperage, against the east Hedge.
  - Depot Three is in the quarry (down the headframe ladder in the clearing). The slag-slope is its back wall.
  - The Conservatory's reed-hall and orchard are through the east wall of the Conservatory Reading Room.
  - The Governor's house is the north-row door beside the Greenhall. The standing-stones are through the second gap in the clearing's east hedge.

## The lived-in world, wave 1: Oakhaven's people (built 7 Nov; ROADMAP R10 W1)

Walk Oakhaven at different hours: early morning (6–8), mid-morning, noon (the bench by the square), afternoon, dusk (19:00, when they go in), the evening (the Boar's door, the window-lamps), and the dead hour (only the watch). Use REST to jump; everyone snaps to where the new hour puts them. Talk to anyone in the street.

- **Busy or crowded?** About 20 people are out by day. Does the square feel alive without feeling like a fair? Is anyone in the way?
- **Whispers.** Overheard lines come every 11 seconds or so at most, from whoever is nearest. Too many? Too few?
- **The walks.** They take real routes round the houses. Does anyone look stuck, or walk oddly (into a fence, a crate, a tree)?
- **The jobs.** Selling, sweeping, hammering, picking, scrubbing, hanging washing, raking, playing. Can you tell what each person is doing from how they move?
- **At home time** they walk to their door and vanish. Does it read as going in?

## The Rite, from procession to pumps (built 5 Nov; ROADMAP R9 item 3)

Book One ch. 9–10 as a played scene. The tests drive every stage headlessly; what they cannot judge is how it feels at a walking pace, how loud it is, and whether the words land on screen. To reach it quickly: a save with the coil taken to Kaelen (`coil_done`), or from the console set `G.flags.coil_done = G.flags.church_come = true` and go to Lyra's door on Cooper's Row.

- **The door.** The point of no return has moved from the shaft gate to Lyra's door: the column forms there, and the card lists what is still open. Does "Not yet." feel like a real pause, and does the shaft gate's "sealed until the Rite" line read right if you try it first?
- **The procession.** Walk with the column up the main road to the headframe. It waits if you fall more than a few houses behind; is that wait felt as the Legionnaire looking back, or as a bug? Press N to hum with the town and watch the kerbs; cast a loud Song twice and let a Legionnaire walk you to the back. The Pure Note is a 528 tone every 2.4 s: too much? Too little? The kerb figures are solid; can you still pass them on the road?
- **The vault.** Three cards, then the needle: a gauge at the foot of the view says BLOCKED or HE CAN SEE THE NEEDLE. Is it clear where to stand? The Inquisitor paces, so you shuffle. Twelve seconds (eight if you were marched). Try both: block it, and stand aside. Standing aside plays an Echo, grey, and puts you back at the start of the needle; the book's Inquisitor never sees.
- **The Hold.** Kaelen goes to the drum; the gauge says ON YOU / ON THE DRUM. Humming (N) within a few paces of the Factor keeps her lenses on you; eight bolts take fifty-one seconds, as in the book. If her lenses settle on the plate, an Echo plays grey and the Hold starts again. Is standing and humming for fifty seconds tense or dull? Is the Echo clear as "that did not happen"?
- **Sing it down.** The falling fifth is a Hold the Pitch (174 to 116 and back, nine seconds). However you do, the Root lets go, as in the book; a poor score only changes your line in the card.
- **The Golems.** Two come, two stand at the rail; keep one off the stool. "Enough." comes when both fall or when the Hold clock runs out. Check the stopped Golems look stopped, not asleep.
- **Sixty seconds.** Aldric counts from the tube (41, 40, 30, 20, 10, 5, 2). Lyra takes four by the wrist; the cage goes up once at about thirty seconds with Tobiah and the sisters. A hand on a pump-man's wrist (use him) holds for three seconds and buys a name; step away and the handle takes him back. The eight who are left are Edders, Ruhl, Caine, Ott, Sennet, Vey, Marrow and Hale B. (the ring and the scar). Is three seconds the right price? Is it clear you cannot save them, only name them?
- **The pour.** Forty thousand gallons, the crack, the green. If you are still on the platform you lose a third of your health and the card says so. Then the Greening escape as before (five minutes, the service ladder, the headframe, the gate, the mast). The journal names how many you took off the handles.
- **The voices (recorded 5 Nov).** 48 lines are recorded with the Kokoro pipeline in tools/voices: Tobiah, the sisters, the Factor, the Inquisitor, Kaelen, Aldric through the speaking-tube, Lyra, and each of the eight pump-men saying his name. Each card plays its lines in the book's order, and picking a choice cuts the rest. "I. Am. Fortunate." is spoken as three words held apart, flat on 174 with a hum under it. Aldric's numbers come through a tinny tube filter. These are synthetic voices, so listen for: does the Fortunate line sound wrong in the right way, or just robotic? Is the Governor's 19-second "Sealing the vault" speech too long to sit through on the tube card? Do the pitch calls every ten seconds get tiresome?
- **Custody (before the Rite):** Lyra now writes the palm message for Rooke on your hand; take his hands in his surgery. `rooke_drops` is set for the Greening walk to use later.
