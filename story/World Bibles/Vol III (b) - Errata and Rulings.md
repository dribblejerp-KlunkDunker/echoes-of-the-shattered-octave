# World Bible Vol III — Errata and Rulings
### Corrections made while drafting Book Three. Where this sheet and Vol III disagree, this sheet wins. The manuscript wins over both.

## Author rulings
- **Casimir Vaelen has been glassed.** He is 140 years old, and glass-laced for 80 of them (series bible canon). Strike Vol III §A.3's "he has never been glassed himself."
  - **Replacement blind spot:** his glass is *laced*, never *set*. For eighty years he has kept it from spreading by never letting it carry a note. It only receives. He listens to the Anchors, and to all Dawning glass, and has never once sung back or held anything.
  - This is why he wants a successor, and why Kaelen, whose glass is set and singing, is one.
  - Line, stated once, late: *"You have listened to them for eighty years, and you have never once sung back."*
- **Five-book structure** per Vol III. Book Two ends at Chapter 14.
- **Book Three dial:** darker, more action, more dread, more death and destruction. Standing rules hold:
  - no death without an heir;
  - damage persists;
  - harm to children is never rendered in detail;
  - the Hush rules apply.

## State-of-play corrections (manuscript wins)
- **Sable** has **one** piece left at the end of Book Two, not two: the smell of the Veil's scriptorium. Her mother's hands went on Wren, and her true name went at the Malting. A **copy** of her name sits in the third tray (taken when the Office swore her in, in 416).
- **Kaelen's tideline:** a finger's width from the throat at the end of Book Two, and half a finger after the Valley (Book Three Ch2). The green Velocity vein runs to the edge of the glass at the throat.
- **Lyra** has no voice at all, not a whisper (Book Two Ch14). Vol III's "whisper" for Book Five should be treated as something hard-won, if it is kept at all.
- **Cores:** 432 at the end of Book Two, **396** after the Tramway (Book Three Ch1).

## Timeline corrections
- **The Rift opened in 413.** The baskets have gone down for **six years**, not eleven: 2,011 knots, one per descent. Command has known for about five years.
- **Fen** was pressed at twelve in summer 413, at Ebb Cross. She looks twelve and is **eighteen**. She was the first survivor on the Shelf.
- **The Valley of Velocity** broke in **398**, not at the Fracture.
- **Schedule F:** disposals before 416 carried rough dockets. Sable redrafted form **F/3** in spring 416, and it is the standard docket since.

## Naming and places
- Vol IV's proposed **Deputy Registrar Halloway** is renamed **Hester Vell**, because Wren Halloway exists.
- **The Hall of Carriage** is "three floors and a basement," counting the street floor as the first:
  - basement: Sorting, and **bin seven** (cracked cores, filed by docket number);
  - first floor: Receiving and Carriage;
  - second floor: Assignment and the **Concordance**;
  - third floor: the carpet corridor and the walnut cabinet of staff trays.
- **Null-Watch:**
  - forty batteries on four miles of obsidian and lead wall;
  - General Theresa Ward, mute since 413, commands in iron-cant; her adjutant is 94;
  - the Lead Brothers;
  - Standing Order Nine.

## New mechanics introduced in Book Three Part One
- **The edge:** within about 30 yards of the Maw's lip, Kaelen's arm goes silent and **Vaelen cannot hear**. It is the only such place. The Quiet pulls anyone who stays.
- **The thing below the Maw** reached for Kaelen's Dawning glass at the Refusal. Kaelen cannot stay at the Watch.
- **"A piece knows its way home, if there's someone home to call it."** Rendered can reclaim their own cracked cores without a scribe's price. Hollows still need a scribe to pay.
- Cores set on **tuning stock** can carry a living trench-voice home with them (Tessa Wray and Annis). This seeds the failure of the tenth press.
