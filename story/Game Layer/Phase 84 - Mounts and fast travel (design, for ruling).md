# Phase 84: mounts and fast travel (design, for the owner's ruling)

From the roadmap: "A pony to hire at the gates (its picture, how it rides, where it will not go); a punt for the Sunk Lanes [PROPOSED]; waypoints at milestones; the world map knowing the roads. Design first, for the owner's ruling, then built." From the world plan (approved): "a pony for the Greenroot road and the drove road, which will not go onto planks, down the Pound Stair or into the Grove; and a punt for the Sunk Lanes." Everything below that is not already in the game is **[PROPOSED]**.

## What the game has now

- **The North Gate stables** in Oakhaven hire "the roan mare" for a day (25 sovereigns, 10 back if returned early). She is not ridden: while she is hired, world-map journeys take about a third less time and you get less hungry on the road. She "waits outside any door you go in by", but nothing shows her.
- **The world map** sends you to any place you have found in your region, in a straight line, one minute a cell (two paces: cautious and quick), with a chance of an ambush on the way that rises at night, in bad weather and on dangerous ground.
- **The roads** are now laid on the chart exactly where they are walked (Phase 82b), with finger-posts at every gate.

## The design

### 1. The pony, ridden [PROPOSED]

- **Where to hire one:** the North Gate stables as now, and two more: the Bellwether's yard in Wethercombe and the Greenroot road's far end (the Long Common's drovers' pen). Same price and terms everywhere; a pony hired at one can be left at any of them.
- **The roan mare becomes a pony you ride.** Press the mount key (M, or the pad's left stick click) beside her to get on or off. Riding, you go about one and three-quarter times as fast as walking, sit a little higher (you see over the hedges and the furze), make more noise than a person creeping and less than one running, and tire much more slowly. You can fight from the saddle with a sword or staff (no bow); a hard hit or a hound at her heels throws you off once in a while.
- **Where she will not go,** as the plan says: onto planks (the footbridges, the lock-gate beams), down the Pound Stair, into the Deep Grove, or indoors (she comes through every gate between one stretch of road and the next, the Downs' stile too: it has a drovers' gate beside it). At such a place she stops and you get off; she waits there. The finger-posts' roads are all ride-able except where they cross planks; every such place has a ford or a way round within a few paces, or the road says why not.
- **Her picture:** a roan pony, drawn in code like the other animals, seen from in front, behind and both sides, walking and trotting; her head and ears at the bottom of the first-person view when you ride. She stands, grazes and swishes her tail when left.
- **If you leave her:** she waits where you got off for six hours, then walks home to the stable she came from (and the hire runs on). The hire still ends after a day.
- **Hounds and her:** hounds go for her if you leave her near their ground at night; she bolts home and the ostler charges for the fright (5 sovereigns). Nothing kills her.

### 2. Milestones, and travel by them [PROPOSED]

- **A milestone at each end of every stretch of road** (beside each finger-post, so about thirty in the Reach), and in each town at its gate or square: a short stone with the place's name cut in it. Touching one (walking past it is enough) marks it on the world map.
- **The world map sends you only to milestones you have touched** and to the towns themselves, and draws the way **along the roads**: the route goes gate to gate through the stretches between, never straight across the country. The time is the length of that road at one minute a cell on foot (as now), about half that riding.
- **Long journeys stop for the night.** A journey that would run past dusk ends at the halt nearest the road's middle (the Three Pines, the Howe's lee, the Landing's inn, a fold-fire) unless you choose to press on in the dark. That is how "two days" on the posts and fourteen hours of road agree: a day's walking, a night under the pines, and the rest of the way.
- **Ambushes happen on the stretch where they happen.** Instead of one roll for the whole journey, each stretch you pass through rolls by its own danger, hour and weather, and if it comes up you arrive on that stretch, mid-road, with what was waiting (and can take the road on from there).

### 3. The punt [PROPOSED]

- On the Sunk Lanes (Phase 85; not built yet). Hired from the punt-woman at Cross Pool for a few coppers. Pole along the lanes' water at walking pace; the lanes' fallen walls and drowned shop-fronts are where you go by punt and nowhere else. Built with the Lanes, in Phase 85, to this design.

## What is not changing

The Kestrel still carries you between countries; nothing walks from the Reach to the Woods or the Coast. Places from earlier parts of the story stay grey on the map. The horse-hire numbers (25, 10 back, a day) stay. A save from before this phase loads with no pony out and every milestone you have walked past already counted.
