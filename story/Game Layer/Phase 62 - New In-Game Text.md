# Phase 62 — ROADMAP R10 wave 2: Whisper-Grove's Hollows keep their loops

*Recorded 8 Nov 2026 from the game's translation file (`tools/i18n/p62-whispergrove.tsv`). Lines 2, 4, 6 and 7 follow Book One ch. 15 (the Weeping Plaza) closely; everything else is [PROPOSED].*

## What is new, in short

- **Canon first.** Whisper-Grove died in a single night in 416 and "went on standing there". Nobody in it lives a day. So its life is the Hollows' loops, and the book's own are kept exactly, all day and night: the woman in the good wool coat between the third pillar and the basin's edge; the two men mid-argument at the corner of the colonnade; the young man washing a plate with no dirt on it.
- **Thirteen more loops** about the city by day [PROPOSED]. At dusk they drift into the Sunken Court "the way leaves do" (canon: "perhaps two hundred of them in the plaza tonight") and stand there through the night.
- **The Office feeder** [PROPOSED], the only living person on the streets: a clerk with a cart at dawn and dusk, putting bread where the loops go (canon: "they feed themselves if food's put where their loop goes. The Office kept four thousand of them alive for two years").
- The plaza's twenty standing Hollows, Halder Wick's table and the lamplighter are unchanged.

## Rulings (8 Nov 2026)

- **The thirteen loops:** keep them all.
- **The Office feeder at dawn and dusk:** keep.

Nothing in this phase is still open.
