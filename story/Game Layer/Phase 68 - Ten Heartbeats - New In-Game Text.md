# Phase 68 — Ten Heartbeats, played (Book One ch. 21)

**Built 10 November 2026** (`js/cutter68.js`; test `test/cutter68.test.js`; ROADMAP R9 item 20). Every new line of in-game text, in English with its French. Status: **canon (book)** for the chapter's words and events (Book One ch. 21, ll. 5481–5658), with the engineer named Pennick as ch. 21 names him; **[PROPOSED]** for the play around them and the lines marked below.

**How it hooks in.** The climb at Whisper-Grove (the mast-ladder, or kestrel16's "Climb, and go on to the Deep Woods") calls `startPart4` in `js/coda.js`, which now hands the passage to `CUTTER68.start()` when the Kestrel's deck can be played. Its old two-card version ("Say nothing. Let him count." / "Cut it, Vane.") stays as the fallback when the deck is switched off (Options, "Aboard the Drift-Kestrel"). The state that starts Part Four moved into `part4State()`, shared by both; the chase ends in coda.js's own `part4Land()`.

**The beats.** Eleven minutes (one card, on the ground); over the side (the deck); the cutter and the horn; pack the crates (the hold); the canopy and the ten heartbeats (the deck); the long gun, the hook and the proctors; "Don't cut it!" and four seconds; the willow; the courier; the deep woods. Then the Deep Woods map as before.

**[PROPOSED] choices.**
- **The ten heartbeats.** Ch. 21 never counts ten heartbeats; the title points at Vane's count off Cape Dissonance (told in Books Two to Four). The game plays the title as the traveller's own count: keep still and quiet (`G.noise`, the game's Narrow, under 1.9 plus 0.15 for each crate packed, so a walk at 2.4 is always heard; past NARROW28's fear line, where your breath is loud anyway, only what you add on top of it counts) for ten heartbeats of 0.9 s while "the cutter went over the top of them at three hundred feet". Whatever you do, it "lost them in the mist and came back … because of the horn", as the book has it: the stillness never changes the chapter.
- **The player's part:** heaving the water, canvas, tackle and chain over the side; packing the three crates in the hold with Hetty (the crew and Hetty do both without you); holding on for Vane's four seconds; and, without the Aegis, meeting two proctors at the rail with Mags and a belaying pin (the old card's line).
- **Echoes** (RITE43.echo, grey, then the moment again): a sound during the count; cutting the line yourself; dying at the rail. Their words are the game's, not the book's.
- **Variants.** Without Bettis awake (put back, or never woken), Pennick's figures, the hold, Hetty's paragraph, the boy's glance, the deck in the dark and Vane's "And one screaming." lose her. Without the Aegis following, the figures, the ladder and the deck in the dark lose it. Without Sable joined, her count of the courier, her explanation and the Mausoleum lines are left out (the old card's convention: the archivist is still aboard).
- **Where she leaves from.** The book's sky-dock is on the Cloister road; the game's ladder is at Whisper-Grove's south landing. The cards keep the book's "came off the Cloister road".
- **Hetty without Bettis:** "Hetty Bram put the nine children in the sail-locker with the door shut and Nell Carder in with them." (the book's sentence without the screaming it answers).

**Left for the owner.** The book's shot clips the **number-three** lift-tank (ch. 21, and Pennick's "number-three tank" in ch. 24). The game's Deep Woods (Phase 16, `js/coda.js` part4Land's journal, Vane, Mags, Tobiah's patch) still says number-two (Vol VIII, Found in Phases 15–25 (B) #24). This scene quotes the book; the later lines were not rewritten.
