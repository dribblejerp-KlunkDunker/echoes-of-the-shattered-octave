# Phase 113: the Rift, a full lore recheck

22 Nov 2026. The owner: "due a full lore recheck then proceed". Five lore readers read every line about the Rift in all four books and every World Bible but Vol VIII, one reader to a file or a shelf of files. Their briefs, about 630 passages with file and line, are kept in `story/Game Layer/Lore briefs/The Rift (22 Nov 2026)/`. I then checked the lines the designs rest on against the books myself.

Short names: **B3P1, B3P2, B3P3** = Book Three, Parts One, Two, Three; **V1, V2, V3, V3b, V6, V7** = World Bibles; **SB** = Series Bible.

## 1. What the recheck added or corrected

**The Quartermaster.**
- He goes out "at the third hour, as he had every night for six years" (B3P2:65), from the postern at Tower Nine's foot, "with the basket on his back and his lantern" (B3P2:67).
- He was "Quartermaster of the old garrison at Null-Watch from 406. Penal legion from the spring of 413" (B3P2:289). He was "kept on at the Watch because nobody else knew where anything was" (B3P2:347). He is sixty and wears a lead coat (B3P2:109).
- His sister keeps a bakery in Greystairs (B3P2:347). She knits. "I had Two-Eleven write to her for it in the summer" (B3P2:75): Two-Eleven writes his letters, and things come to the Watch "on the cart".
- The bread is counted by the head, and the count comes up from below: "It was four hundred and twenty-six, Fen says" (B3P2:75).
- The books never say what else he packs or where the bread is baked.

**The glass.** "Within about thirty yards of the lip, Dawning glass goes silent and Vaelen cannot hear. It is the only such place on the continent." (V6:141; the scene is B3P1:1011). So the glass hears on the wall-walk and does not hear out on the grey rock.

**Rot at the Rift.** The Series Bible holds both at once: "The dead do not rot here, until suddenly everything does." (SB:228). This is the owner's ruling of 22 Nov in the canon's own words: nothing rots, and a gravewell is where everything suddenly does.

**Where the carts tip.** No book and no volume shows it: only "disposed of at the lip" (B3P2:601), "Carts. The lip" (B3P3:163), "They tipped the cart" (B3P1:1319). The survivors land on the Shelf, and the Shelf lies under Batteries Eight, Nine and Ten (B3P1:2066, 2102). **So the carts tip above the Shelf, by Batteries Eight to Ten.** My Design C had the tipping stage "north along the wall from Tower Nine", away from the Shelf. That was wrong: it must be by Eight to Ten.

**Throat-Wraiths** are in Vol II and Vol I (V2:482, V1:500), and neither ties them to the Rift. The Unfinished at the tip is the game's own placing [PROPOSED], as the ruling of 22 Nov allows.

**The time.** Book Three's chapters at the Rift fall in the middle of Ashfall. The game stands about a month before, not "a season" as Phase 100's record says.

## 2. Phase 100, checked against the recheck

Nothing built in Phase 100 is contradicted. Three small things:
- Its record says "a season before the book". It is about a month. The record's wording is corrected in Vol VIII's entry for this phase; no game line says it.
- The Quartermaster's past can now be said truer: quartermaster of the old garrison since 406, condemned in 413 and kept on. Design A uses it.
- The mooring-yard is "on the west side of the Watch ... as far from the lip as the Watch goes" (B3P1:1872), with the stair up to the wall-walk between Five and Six (B3P1:859). Phase 100 has it so.

## 3. Contradictions in the books, for the owner

These are in the manuscript or between volumes. None blocks Design A. I have not changed any book.

1. **The wall's length.** "Four miles" (B3P1:767, 1137, 1143; V6:115) against "Forty miles of wall, every battery marked" on the General's map (B3P1:2102, 1864). Forty towers at two hundred yards is four and a half miles. The game has four.
2. **How far down the Shelf is.** "Three hundred feet below the lip" (B3P1:1243) against the Quartermaster's "You'll see the Shelf at two hundred" (B3P1:1219). Both can be true (seen at two hundred, reached at three), and the game never goes down.
3. **The knots.** Vane counts 2,011 (B3P1:927); the Quartermaster says "two thousand and fourteen times" the same week (B3P1:1215). Six years of nights is about 2,190. The game gives no number.
4. **The Shelf's count.** 379, 365, 364, 363 and "about four hundred" all appear (B3P2:75, 665; B3P3:197, 223, 119). The game gives no number.
5. **Who commands.** Vol II has "Garrison-Commander Nine-Four ('Ferris')" and command rotating every ninety days (V2:426, V1:335); the manuscript has General Theresa Ward, six years in post, with 94 as her voice. The game follows the manuscript.
6. **Two-Eleven.** Vol II's is a battery-sergeant of nineteen years (V2:427); the manuscript's is the big slow trooper with the dog. The game follows the manuscript.
7. **The garrison's size.** Vol II says about eight thousand (V2:412); the manuscript gives no count.
8. **The Null-Amalgam.** Vol I and II have it as a thing that already walks, "wearing ... two hundred and six Null-Watch personnel" (V1:531, V2:545); the Series Bible has it "forming below the Maw" and "growing in the dark" (SB:141, 228). **This matters for Phase 114**, whose plan makes it Redoubt Zero's keeper. See section 5.
9. **Redoubt Zero** is in Vol II and the Series Bible only (V2:418, SB:228): "the closest permanent structure to the Maw", "guards the southern lip", ninety-day rotations. Book Three never names it. The causeway and the rotation road of the Phase 90 plan are in no book and no volume.
