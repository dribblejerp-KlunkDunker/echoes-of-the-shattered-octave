# Phase 95: the Dawning Heights, part three: the Pilgrim Chain and the Altar (text for review)

*Written 16 Nov 2026 on Fable 5.2, on the owner's word that day: "I accept all, merge and proceed". Code: `js/pilgrim95.js`. Test: `test/pilgrim95.test.js` (24 checks). Every line is new and [PROPOSED] unless a book or bible is named; the French is in `tools/i18n/p95-pilgrim.tsv`.*

## The book changed the plan

Phase 89 and Phase 90 drew this place before Book Two's chapters 13 and 14 were re-read for it. The book wins on each point:

1. **The chain's city end is the seventh anchor-house,** on the south-east rim: "A squat stone tower on the rim like the others, but whole, with a slate roof, because the Church had kept it", with the winch and two Flambeaux "keeping the winch for the Church". Phase 90's "pilgrims' hostel" became the old robing-room under it, inside the city's frame.
2. **There is no shelter on the chain.** "The cage is the shadow. It's the only roof on that whole chain." So the "half-way canopy-station" is the cage itself, standing on its cable at the half-way mark.
3. **The shortcut is the cage on its cable,** not a rope-slide: the cable runs "beside the walkway on iron rollers, a kilometre of it, to a second drum on Lumina".
4. **The Bleach parts round glass-fire.** "Each man in crimson came along the chain in a little moving bubble of ordinary shade." On the chain a Flambeau's fire is shade.
5. **Glass-fire burns on its bearer's faith.** Crane's censer gutters as his faith goes, and two dead men's censers burn on "because their faith had not had time to go out". So the keeper is not killed: his fire goes out.
6. **Night is the safer time.** By day the white "was joined by a second light… and the two of them together were more than anything could stand in." And Vol VII: "The dead hour is the hour before dawn."
7. **The Church is not at the Altar in Harvestmonth.** It "had come back to the Altar" six days before Brenna Corliss says so, in Ashfall, and it holds the Pure Note day and night only "wherever the Church prepares a Rite". So nobody holds the note here in Harvestmonth. What the Altar has every day is its own people: the clerics who polish the Eye "every day for seven years with oil and ash", and the choir at the dawn office (Book Three: the Pure Note is "sounded at the Altar every dawn by the Church's choirs").

## What was built

- **The way in.** An iron stair in the east wall of Bardo's winch-room (the Anchorage) goes up to the robing-room.
- **The robing-room** (the first lamp). Four hundred white robes on their pegs, canopies stacked, a wash-copper. **Perpetua Lisle** [PROPOSED], the robing-woman, has six things to say and sells a pilgrim's robe.
- **The seventh anchor-house.** The iron ring in the floor with the chain going down through it, the winch (the book's own: the drum the height of a man, the brake-wheel, the lever as long as an oar), two **Flambeaux of the winch**, a barred door to the top tier's white streets that never opens, and the mouth. The Flambeaux let nobody out of the mouth but the choir and its bearers (the game's own line, not the book's): in a pilgrim's robe the mouth is open, because they look at the robe and not at you.
- **The Pilgrim Chain.** A kilometre of black link, an iron deck with a rail at either hand, the cable on its rollers, the Spire on your right hand, the peaks below. The whole of it is in the Bleach, which takes you by the hour: three health a second by day, one and a half by night, and six tenths in the dead hour. A card shows the hour and whether you are in shade.
- **The first span.** Eight parties of the Altar's clerics go out and back under their canopies on the place's own clock (the same for everyone, never chance). Under a canopy the light goes on round you. The Tiers' rule is the rule: "Walk in the shade of the one ahead of you. If there is no one ahead of you, run."
- **The cage** (the second lamp), at the half-way mark, with a bearer's cowl left on its bench.
- **The second span.** No canopies. Four **Flambeaux of the Chain** walk it, each in the shade of his own censer. You can fight in a man's shade or walk in it: in the whites they take you for a bearer until you are close. Put down, a Flambeau sits against the rail and his censer burns on for forty-five seconds; then it goes out.
- **The Glass-Grown's slope** (a secret, down an iron ladder). Eleven of them stand on Lumina's shoulder facing the Eye, in the stripe of the chain's shadow, which falls the wrong way. They are never fought. Walk and they stand; run, or sing, and every one turns its head. A pair of pilgrim's sandals lies at the head of the slope.
- **The chain's far end: the keeper, Brother-Captain Aurel Sant** [PROPOSED, Phase 89]. He holds the lowest step with a fire-stave. He throws glass-fire where you stand: it burns where it lies, and for a few steps round it the white stands off, so the only shade in the fight is his. 200 health; he hits for 7 to 13. When his fire goes out he kneels, and leaves the Dark Stave and his warrant, once. After a week his fire is lit again.
- **The Lumina winch** (the shortcut). While his fire stands the winch-men keep you from the drum. After it, you take the brake off, and the cage rides both ways between the half-way mark and the winch.
- **The great steps and the Altar's rim** (the third lamp), under one ring of black canopies.
- **The Altar, seen and never entered.** A line of seven Flambeaux stands across every gap between the pillars. Beyond them: the white disc, the Vessel's stone (empty), the old chapels with their people "seven years thin", and the Eye on its frame, white as the inside of a shell but for the cartwheel of bright bronze at its centre, with a cleric polishing it. In the hour after the dead hour the Altar choir stands under its canopies and sounds the Pure Note: the dawn office. At every other hour the canopies stand empty.
- **The Souls rules.** Three shade-lamps (group j). A Piece here rings at 528.
- **The gear: a seventeenth set, the Pilgrim Chain.** Pilgrim's Robe (Perpetua sells it), Bearer's Cowl (in the cage), Pilgrim's Sandals (the slope), the Dark Stave (the keeper's). Three pieces: the Flambeaux of the Chain take you for a bearer until you are close. All four: leaving shade, the Bleach waits three seconds before it begins on you. Each has its own look and picture.

## Phase 92 matched to the books a third and fourth time

The lore readers of Phase 96 (see below) and this phase's own reading found six more things in the Umbral Tiers. All are fixed:

1. **Glass-fire is fire, not the Bleach.** Phase 89 had said "where it burns, the Bleach can follow". Book Two shows the opposite. The Lamplighter's fire now burns as fire, and the three lines that said the light follows it are rewritten.
2. **The bell hangs in the black tower, below the line.** "From the Basilica's black tower, a deep slow bronze note"; "Below them, in the black tower, a bell began to ring". The sign at the bell-gallery stair said the bell was above the line.
3. **There is no cloud under the city.** "Two thousand feet of dark air, and the peaks at the bottom of it pale as teeth under the stars." The gulf's floor is now drawn as dark air with the peaks' snow, and the words "cloud" and "cloud deck" are gone from the Tiers.
4. **No winch-boats, no valley farms.** The only supply the books show is by skyship into the cradles ("a Church packet… a pair of battered freighters"). The two winch-boats are removed, and the harbourmaster now says: "The freighters come into the cradles, and the ladders go up from here. Everything the city eats comes past me, and I count it."
5. **The Bleach has no source.** It comes "from nowhere and from everywhere". Brenna's line that mirrors are "what the Bleach shines from, up on the Walks" now reads: "The Altar permits one mirror in all the Heights, and it is not this one."
6. **The city hangs from the Spire's flank,** not from the mountain's face (the arrival line).

## What the lore readers found for the rest of the Heights (Phase 96)

An army of readers checked every remaining Heights place against the books before anything was built. Their findings are in five records beside this one. In short:
- **The White Mile:** on hold for your ruling. The Foundry is on the Spire, not in the city; the Unison hunts by sound, not sight; the Eye is "the only mirror the Altar permitted in all the Heights", which sets Book Two against Vol II's Mirror Walks and so against the Polisher. Three whole designs to choose from are in "Phase 96 - The White Mile (findings and options, for ruling)".
- **The nine anchor-houses:** withdrawn. The books know only the sixth and the seventh; nobody lives in the Bleach.
- **The cloud deck and the valley farms:** withdrawn. The book has no cloud under the city and nothing growing below it.
- **The Bell Yard:** that name is a Whisper-Grove street in Book One. The place is being built as the book's black square before the Basilica's west end, with the black tower and its bell.
- **The Basilica school:** the bench under the east window is above the line, in the sealed nave. The school is being built as a schoolroom on the shade side [PROPOSED].
- **Sun-Peak Vigil and the Chalk Cliffs:** these stand on the bibles alone. They are being built small, without the under-stair, the "thirty-eight years" or the Light's lullaby, which the books contradict or would give away early.

## For the owner

1. **The robing-room and Perpetua Lisle** stand in for the "pilgrims' hostel". *Recommend: keep.*
2. **The robe opens the mouth.** The Flambeaux pass anyone in a pilgrim's robe. *Recommend: keep.*
3. **The cage rests at the half-way mark** between offices, with the lamp under its silk. The rest is invented; the cage and the cable are the book's. *Recommend: keep.*
4. **Flambeaux at the Altar in Harvestmonth.** The book has Flambeaux on the chain only on the Rite's morning. The Brother-Captain, his four on the second span and his seven at the pillars rest on Phase 89's approved "Brother-Captain Aurel Sant, who holds the chain's far end". *Recommend: keep, or say if the Altar should stand unguarded before the Church comes back.*
5. **The numbers:** the Bleach's three rates; eight parties; a censer burning forty-five seconds; Sant's 200 health and a cast of fire every five and a half seconds. *Recommend: keep, and tune after you have walked it.*
6. **The dead hour is the thinnest hour.** Vol VII fixes the hour; that the white lies thinnest in it is Phase 90's invention. *Recommend: keep.*
7. **The seventh anchor-house holds two chains:** the seventh of the nine (the Anchorage's "seventh bone") and the Pilgrim Chain, a tenth ("Nine of them… And one more"). The book describes only the Pilgrim Chain in the seventh, and the game has built both. *Recommend: rule that it holds both, so nothing built changes.*
8. **The set's name and bonuses.** *Recommend: keep.*
9. **The six withdrawals and changes of Phase 96** listed above. *Recommend: accept the withdrawals; choose a White Mile.*
10. **Does the player belong on the chain at all?** The independent reviewer of this phase raised it, and it is yours to rule. Book Two: "Nobody had walked it since the Fracture, except the Church, and only under canopies." Here the player walks it a month before the book's people do, passing as a bearer, and frees the Lumina brake to ride the cage. The cage, the cable and the two brakes are the same machine Vane uses in chapter 14, though what the player does with it is not what he does (the player never stops the cable, uncouples the cage or runs the chain under fire). Phases 89 and 90 approved the place and its shortcut. *Recommend: keep the place; if the cage ride feels too near chapter 14, say so and the shortcut becomes a plain gate at the half-way mark.*
11. **The canopies at the Altar's rim before the Rite.** Vane says the choir's poles "are at Lumina now", which reads as moved there for the Rite. The one ring here in Harvestmonth, and the daily dawn office under it, rest on one line of Book Three ("sounded at the Altar every dawn by the Church's choirs"). *Recommend: keep, or say if the rim should stand bare until Ashfall.*
