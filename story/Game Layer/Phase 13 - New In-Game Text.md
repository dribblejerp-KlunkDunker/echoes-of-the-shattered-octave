## P13 NIGHT: new player-facing text (all [PROPOSED])

## Interrupted sleep (screen "The sleep breaks", button "Wake"); one of eight, chosen by the seeded stream
1. You dream the Hedge-gate reed. You are waiting for it to sound. It is a long wait, and you are patient, and then you are not asleep.
2. In the dream the river is talking to itself. Then it is not. You wake with your mouth open on the next word.
3. A hearth cracks somewhere in the dark. You listen for the next crack, and it does not come, and you wake listening.
4. Someone is humming in the next room. The hum stops in the middle of a note, as politely as a guest.
5. You are counting names. You reach the one after yours, and there is nothing there to count.
6. Rain on the roof. Then rain on no roof. Then you.
7. Somebody sets a cup down. You never hear it land.
8. Boots in the grass, coming nearer. Then grass.

## The wake
- Danger: You are awake before you know why. Something near you is already awake, and has been for a while.
- False alarm, fire: Nothing. The fire has gone out, and the dark has only the ordinary silence of dark.
- False alarm, no fire: Nothing. Whatever woke you has gone quiet, which is not quite the same as gone.
- Cat: {cat} hisses, low and flat, a breath before you hear it.
- Companion: {name} is already on their feet. "Easy. Something moved."
- After either: You did not sleep the night through.

## The Unrung Bell
- In Oakhaven: A bell is struck somewhere past the Hedge. The air leans toward the sound, and there is none.
- Outside the Hedge: A bell is struck, close. The dark takes the sound before it can be one.
- Caption: [a bell that does not ring]
- Townsfolk, the day after (generic townsfolk only): Did you hear it last night? The bell. The one that does not. / Something rang in the night and nothing came of it. I have stopped trying to say it better. / I counted the gap after the stroke. It was exactly the length of a bell. / My dog went under the bed. He does not do that for weather. / If a bell rings and you cannot hear it, was it a bell? I would sooner not know. / The hounds were bold after. They always are, after.

## Options

## Lore notes for the author
- The Deacon and his clapperless bell are met in the Deep Woods AFTER the Greening (js/wilds.js). Before it, the bell is heard from "past the Hedge" and never attributed: no Quiet Faith, no Tongueless, no Deacon named in any line. That is an implication, not a statement; the author may prefer a different source (or none). The Bell stops at the Greening.
- The Hush is never described; it is the absence of a bell's sound. No hound, Quiet or boss is ever the wake's enemy (a Quiet is excluded from the table on purpose).
