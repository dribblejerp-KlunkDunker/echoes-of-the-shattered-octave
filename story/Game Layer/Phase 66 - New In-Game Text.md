# Phase 66 — ROADMAP R10 wave 4: the small places keep the hours

*Recorded 8 Nov 2026 from the game's translation file (`tools/i18n/p66-small.tsv`). Every person and line here is [PROPOSED]. The places, the trades and the rules they live by are canon or already in the game.*

## What is new, in short

- **Stillwater Landing.** By day: an eel-trapper at the pier end, a boy fishing, a reed-cutter carrying Flume-Reed, and a washerwoman. After ten at night: a lookout at the lane head, and two men carrying crates off a boat to the fence's lean-to. The eel-traps come from the Festivals addendum (the Eel-Run), and the Flume-Reed from reedcraft.
- **The Low Rings.** Three people by day, near the gangway and well away from the drones and briar-sprites. All gone by four.
- **The Bell Yard and Ashlane.** Canon: nobody lives a day in Whisper-Grove, so its life is the Hollows' loops. There are six more of them: a joiner, a cooper, a carter, a rug-beater, a running boy, and a woman reading the name-boards (Aye and Renn are canon names on Ashlane). At dusk they go out under the archway to the Sunken Court, as in wave 2.
- **The Hollow.** Canon: the burl-sick; thirty-one households, eighty-four souls, nineteen of them children; the pencilled doors; Rooke's book; the Grafters' wagon on Rootday mornings. Nine people, all whispering under the Hush. They go indoors while the wagon is in.
- **The clearing.** Canon: the Consortium's headframe and roster, the night shift, timber out of the clearing with Tomas, and the gate shut at dusk. There are three quarry hands on the dawn-to-dusk shift, two sawyers, and a woman gathering. They are quiet, because "out here nothing eats anything" (Tomas, Phase 9).

## Questions for sleeves

1. **The Landing's smugglers after dark.** Keep, or is the Landing quieter than that at night?
2. **The Hollow's children.** One child playing "Quietest", and a girl carrying her brother. Is that the right tone for the Hollow, or should it have no children on the lanes?
3. **The quarry hands.** Should they go in and out through the North Gate (the town's gate), or does the quarry shift live at the quarry?
