## What is new, in short

- **The Governor's house (R5 #5)** (`js/governor46.js`). Kaelen's birthplace, above the square (Book One ch. 2): the step the town tells both ways, the long table laid for one, the lease worn soft at the signature and the famine winter's list, Aldric's dressing-table (the hidden roots), and upstairs the aired room, aired again in autumn. Open Parts One and Two.
- **The standing-stones (R5 #6)** (`js/stones47.js`). The ring painted on the clearing's eastern skyline, reached by a second gap in the east hedge beside the Greenroot road's (Phase 20-21 Author Rulings). Eight stones on a low rise; Nullday's lies flat, so the skyline shows seven.
- **Hearthday at the Boar & Dulcimer, played (R9 item 5)** (`js/boar48.js`). Book One ch. 4 as a played scene. The book's lines are kept close; the count, the hounds through the gaps, Last Rites, the sleeve, Second Opinion, Vane's laugh, the boots, the clerk and the four rules are canon.
- **The water-meadow, played (R9 item 4)** (`js/meadow49.js`). Book One ch. 6 as a played scene: Nell, the crossing, the culvert, the shape at the tree-line in Lyra's old voice, the whistle, the calling-song, Lyra's torn answer, Tam's walk, the stool and the dead hour are canon.

| 27 | It gives up the whistle and begins a different song: the calling-song, in Lyra's whole voice, perfect and sweet. | 32 | The hounds go past you. | 33 | He misses the high note. | 34 | Lyra is through. Go back under the Hedge. | Lyra est passée. Repassez sous la Haie. |

| 37 | At the dead hour the Hedge talks, as it does every night. At the very end, just before dawn, softly, in the Narrow, it whistles. Two notes. The Root, and down. Exactly right. | À l'heure morte, la Haie parle, comme chaque nuit. | 41 | The water-meadow: the Hedge whistled Pim's whistle at the Listening Hour and he went through the culvert. Lyra and I went after him.
