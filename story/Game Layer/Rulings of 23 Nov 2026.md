## Still open (not ruled today)

One question sits beside the one ruled, and Phase 114's designs will turn on it. The Series Bible and Vol IV make the Null-Amalgam **the Hush's** body, and Vol IV, 1.8, rule 5 says of the Hush: "It has never once been fought successfully, and no scene should imply that it could be." Vol II files the Amalgam under "Class VI, Apex and Unique", with hit points, as a thing that is fought. The Phase 89 plan (question 9) recommended fighting it at Redoubt Zero, "with the fallback that it becomes a thing to cross, like the Drift of Wells, if the owner reads it as the Hush's". Today's ruling settles that it walks. It does not settle whether the player beats it, survives it or crosses it. The designs will offer those.

## A reading to confirm

Found the same morning, after the ruling, while starting Phase 114's brief. **Book Three has a thing under the Shelf that is not named and does not walk.** "Something so large that the word did not apply, the way the word *large* does not apply to a mountain. Something down in the dark at the bottom of the gash at the end of the world turned over, the way a sleeper turns over when a door bangs in the house." (Book Three, Part One, line 1153.) "*Six years it has slept down there under the Refusal, and turned over every dawn when the guns fired, and settled.*" (line 1183.) Part Three calls it "the thing under the Maw, turning over at the Refusal" (line 537).

The Series Bible had given that sleeper the Null-Amalgam's name ("a body forming below the Maw"). Vol I and Vol II's Null-Amalgam is nine and a half tonnes and walks, and Vol I says of it: "It is not the final threat. **It is the Maw's immune response**, and the fact that the Maw has one implies the Maw is alive".

So the ruling and the manuscript both stand only if they are **two things**, and that is how Series Bible v1.6 is now worded:

1. **The Null-Amalgam** (Vol I, Vol II): at the Maw's lip, walking, the Maw's immune response. Phase 114 may show it.
2. **The sleeper below** (Book Three): what the Hush feeds through the bores. Unnamed. Asleep in Harvestmonth 419. Never shown, never fought; the wall feels it turn over at the dawn Refusal.

If the owner means them to be one thing, the manuscript wins by his standing rule, the Amalgam is asleep under the Shelf in Harvestmonth 419, and Phase 114 cannot put it at Redoubt Zero. The two-things wording then comes back out of the Series Bible.
