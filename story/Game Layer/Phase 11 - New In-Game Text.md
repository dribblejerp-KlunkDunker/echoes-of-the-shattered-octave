## Birth signs [PROPOSED] (each +5 to one attribute; None is the default)
- Ionian, the Open Door: The bright, plain mode a child learns first. Doors open a little easier for you.
- Dorian, the Cloister Walk: A minor mode with one stubborn bright note. You keep hope without making a fuss of it.
- Phrygian, the Hound's Step: Dark, tight and quick off the mark. You are light on your feet when the floor goes quiet.
- Lydian, the Floating Light: A bright mode with one note held a little too high. Things tend to land on your side.
- Mixolydian, the Road-Song: A traveller's mode, major with a flat seventh that never resolves. You walk fast.
- Aeolian, the Mourner's Hour: The natural minor, slow and long-breathed. You outlast things.
- Locrian, the Broken Fifth: The mode with no true fifth, and no rest in it. You notice what is missing.
- Bonuses: Ionian Personality, Dorian Willpower, Phrygian Agility, Lydian Luck, Mixolydian Speed, Aeolian Endurance, Locrian Intelligence.
