# Phase 96: The Anchor-Houses (findings, for ruling)

Team E, 16 Nov 2026. Nothing was built. This page says why, and asks one question.
"B2" means `story/Books/Book Two - The Great Discord/Part Two Ch8-14.md`. Line numbers are the file's own.

## What the plan proposed

- Phase 90 l.136: "**The nine anchor-houses:** each: a winch-room, a crew-bunk, a tally-wall; the seventh is the Anchorage's gate."
- Phase 89 l.277: "**The nine anchor-houses** (one is canon, "the seventh"). Each its own small stronghold on the rim, each with its own crew and its own tally."

## What the book says

1. **The rim is a wall, at the top, in the Bleach.** B2 l.1243: "The rim was a wall. A great curved parapet of white stone running round the top of the inverted cone of the city ... and set into it, at intervals, the anchor-houses: squat towers of stone built over the great iron rings where the chains came down out of the sky". The line is at the ninth tier (B2 l.939); the rim is the very top (B2 l.1241). Nobody lives there.
2. **Only two anchor-houses are in the books: the sixth and the seventh.** No other is numbered, placed or described. "Nine anchor-houses" is in no book or bible; it is the plan's. The count is never given.
3. **The sixth is one roofless ring of wall, partly over nothing.** B2 l.1245: "The sixth anchor-house had no roof." B2 l.1957: "a ring of broken wall forty feet across, its roof long gone ... built out over the drop on stone corbels, so that the south-west quarter of its floor had nothing under it but the whole height of Sol-Luminary and the gulf ... a doorway with no door". No winch, crew, bunk or tally.
4. **The seventh is the only whole one, and it is the Church's.** B2 l.2837: "A squat stone tower on the rim like the others, but whole, with a slate roof, because the Church had kept it." It has one room with the winch (B2 l.2839) and two Flambeaux "keeping the winch for the Church" (B2 l.2851). No crew, no bunk, no tally.
5. **Nobody walks the nine.** B2 l.2357: "nobody walked on them who was not mad or a Tiers child on a dare."
6. **Nothing is said of rooms under any anchor-house.** The chain only goes "on down through the floor into the city's frame" (B2 l.1957; l.2837 "like the others").
7. **The crews are not on the rim.** Vol II l.88 gives "The Chain-Yards ... crews on three-month contracts and a 1-in-9 mortality rate", with no anchor-houses. Vol VIII l.2272 (ruled) put the Chain-Yards inside the frame, "worked from underneath since the Fracture" (built as `js/anchorage94.js`).

## Why the place is withdrawn

- Winch-rooms, crew-bunks and tally-walls in nine houses, each a stronghold with its own crew, are contradicted by points 3 to 7. The book wins.
- The seventh is already built (`js/pilgrim95.js`, map `p95_house`, with the robing-room under it [PROPOSED]). That is the plan's "Anchorage's gate", done.
- The sixth is where Book Two's climax happens on 12 Ashfall: Solenne's nest, the Unison, the chain rung a hair flat (B2 l.1249 to 2163). It stays closed in Harvestmonth 419.
- The other seven have nothing in the books but "like the others" (B2 l.2837). Rooms under them would be invention with no line behind it. Team E's earlier idea of under-rooms in the frame is withdrawn too.

So there is nothing left to build that the lore allows. The crews, their tally and the notched link already live in the Anchorage (Phase 94).

## The open question: is the Pilgrim Chain a tenth chain, and what does the seventh hold?

The book counts the Pilgrim Chain apart from the nine:
- B2 l.1739: "the chains. Nine of them, black, a kilometre each. And one more, thinner, lower, almost level, running out from the city's rim to the south-east".
- B2 l.2355: "The Pilgrim Chain was not like the other nine."

So it is a tenth chain. What the book does not settle is whether the seventh anchor-house holds it **alone**, or **also holds the seventh of the nine**.

For "alone":
- B2 l.2837 shows one chain in the seventh: "The chain came up into it out of the white across the gulf from Lumina, almost level, and went through the floor". No slanting bone is mentioned.
- B2 l.2357: the Pilgrim Chain "ran almost level out from the city's south-east rim at the seventh anchor-house".

For "with a bone":
- The sixth anchor-house holds "the sixth chain" (B2 l.1239, l.1957), so house number seems to match bone number.
- B2 l.1243: the houses are "built over the great iron rings where the chains came down out of the sky". The Pilgrim Chain does not come down; it "came up into it ... almost level" (l.2837).
- Book Three `Part One Ch1-4.md` l.585, "The Pilgrim Chain and its anchor-houses", may count the Altar end's winch-house as a second anchor-house. Unclear.

**The game has built both readings at once.** `js/anchorage94.js` has "the seventh bone", one of the nine, tolling in the change ("When the seventh speaks", l.76 to 81). `js/heights92.js` l.97 runs the seventh chain's goods-cage "toward the seventh anchor-house". `js/pilgrim95.js` l.74 puts the Pilgrim Chain through the seventh's ring: "The Pilgrim Chain goes down through it into the city's frame."

**Recommendation:** rule that the seventh holds both: the seventh bone coming down from the Spire, and the Pilgrim Chain coming in level from Lumina. The book does not forbid it, house number matching chain number (the sixth) supports it, and it keeps all three built modules true without a change. It also gives nine houses for nine bones. If the owner rules "alone", then there are ten anchor-houses, and anchorage94's "seventh bone" and heights92's cage line need a new house to run to. Either way, one sentence in Vol VIII should fix it, marked [PROPOSED].

## Three bible slips (the book wins; for the owner to correct)

1. **Vol II l.85:** "Hung from the face of Mt. Lumina on sky-chains ... Six tiers". Book Two hangs the city "from the Spire's flank" (B2 l.545), and it has more than six tiers: the line is "at the ninth tier" (B2 l.939), with the Glassmarket "on the fourth tier above the line" (B2 l.1013). Phase 89 l.196 repeats "the face of Mount Lumina".
2. **Series Bible l.511 and l.551:** the Restoration "on Lumina, the first of Frostmonth". Book Two puts it on "Dawnday the thirteenth of Ashfall" (B2 l.2829), and Vol VII l.106 and l.139 agree: "13 Ashfall | Dawnday | The Restoration on Mount Lumina".
3. **The link has two lengths.** B2 l.545: "Each link as long as a ship's boat". B2 l.1247: "each link as long as a man was tall". The game should pick one; "a ship's boat" is said twice (l.545 and l.2053), so we suggest that one.

## For ruling

- Withdraw "the nine anchor-houses" as a place: yes or no.
- The seventh: holds both chains (recommended), or the Pilgrim Chain alone.
- The three slips: correct in the bibles, yes or no.
