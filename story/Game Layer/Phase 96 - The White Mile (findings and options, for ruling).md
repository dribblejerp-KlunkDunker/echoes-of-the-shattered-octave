# Phase 96: the White Mile (findings and options, for ruling)

16 Nov 2026. The build is on hold until you rule. Every quote is from the team A lore brief
(`army96/a/LORE-whitemile.md`), with file and line. Short names: **B2P2** = Book Two, Part Two Ch8-14; **B1Pro** = Book
One, Prologue; **SB** = Series Bible; **V1, V2, V3** = World Bibles.

## 1. What the plan proposed

Phase 90 (lines 113 to 126) has eight rooms: (1) the line's edge and the scavengers' shelf, the only rest; (2) the
scavengers' lamp; (3) the noble houses, Sancterre and Valmont-Lune; (4) the Unison's walk ("hide, be silent, break their
sight"); (5) the Foundry's gallery, a secret ("the third platform, where it happened"); (6) the Mirror Walks, with gantries
on rails; (7) the great mirror and its keeper, **the Polisher**, who keeps one mirror bright, and whose beam crosses the Walk
until you reach him and cover it; (8) the polishing gantry, the shortcut. No lamps inside. The screen is overexposed. A
cloth on a pole shows where the light is too strong.

## 2. What the book says

**Stands:**
- The look. "Everything inside that mile was overexposed... the eye slid off it and could not find an edge" (B2P2:533).
- The Unison, forty of them, hidden from and not fought. "forty bodies, one mind... hunting their lost lead for seven years" (V3:18).
- The noble houses are real buildings: "House Aubade's tall cold rooms on the Upper Tiers" (B1Pro:36). Sancterre and Valmont-Lune collide with no canon name.
- Scavenging, at the edge only: "where the Tiers go up to the line to scavenge" (B2P2:735).
- No rest inside. Nothing in the book rests above the line.

**Falls:**
- **The Foundry's gallery cannot be a room here.** The Foundry is "at the foot of the Dawning Spire" (B1Pro:11), "thrown up against the Spire's flank" (B1Pro:34), not in the city. The plan also mixes two places. The third platform is on the bore-crew's gantry "halfway up the Spire" (B2P2:1355, 1363). The gallery is the nobility's, "behind a rail of gilded iron" (B1Pro:64). No book shows the Foundry as it is in 419.
- **"Break their sight" is wrong.** The Unison hunts by sound (SB:551). Forty of them stood "ten feet away, in the full white light... They could not hear him" and went away (B2P2:1163-1171). Silence saves you, not cover. They "don't hear words" (B2P2:1051). They follow their own note, sung back (B2P2:1137).
- **The Bleach does not shine from mirrors.** Its light is "sourceless... from nowhere and from everywhere, from the stone itself and the air" (B2P2:947), and "comes from no source" (SB:551).
- **The great mirror, its beam and the Polisher clash with the Eye.** The Eye is "the only mirror in the Heights that the Altar permits" (B2P2:1683). It "had been the only mirror the Altar permitted in all the Heights; the only second light" (B2P2:2399). Even the Eye has gone "a pale dull white like the inside of a shell", except where clerics polish it daily "with oil and ash" (B2P2:2399). That polishing work is already in the game (js/pilgrim95.js:151). A beam needs the sun: the Eye "at dawn... takes the first sun and throws it" (B2P2:1683). The night light casts no beam.
- **What ran the Walks has been struck.** Vol III strikes the Under-Choirs (V3:18, V3:236), and with them V2:522, "The Mirror Walks still run on them".

**Added by the book:**
- **The way in.** "You came up the last ladder through a hanging house that had been gutted and left — a dark room with nothing in it, black walls, a black-painted window — and out through its roof onto a walkway" (B2P2:941). It is on the ninth tier. The offerings lie on the line itself (B2P2:955-957).
- **The old Glassmarket** "on the fourth tier above the line" (B2P2:1013): "White baskets of white fruit on white trestles under white awnings. And among the stalls... the people" (B2P2:1019). They are "seven years thin" (B2P2:1023), and some are "still reaching for the coin" (B2P2:1025). The book's warning: "Don't touch them" (B2P2:1015, 1027).
- **How one crosses.** "from shadow to shadow, the way you cross a river on stones" (B2P2:985). Gaps run two to five strides (B2P2:985, 1009). Every shadow points away from the Spire (B2P2:947). The light takes colour from the skin in two seconds, and "It did not hurt" (B2P2:975-977). Black cloth is wound over the face and black gloves are worn (B2P2:953). "Keep your voice down... Everything up there can hear it" (B2P2:965).
- **Night and day.** By night there is "a flat sourceless white glow"; "It did not warm... and it hummed" (B2P2:947). By day the sun joins it, and "the two of them together were more than anything could stand in" (B2P2:1905). At noon the shade is "the width of a man's shoulders" (B2P2:1909). "The Tiers slept in the morning; they lived at night" (B2P2:2911).
- **The Unison's procession.** "in four ranks of ten, coming slowly down the white street toward the plaza" (B2P2:1103). They climb a white house after a sound, through its trapdoor and stair, and give up (B2P2:1099-1171).

## 3. Three designs to choose from

**(a) The book's mile, with a keeper who owes nothing to mirrors.**
- *Rooms:*
  1. The gutted house on the ninth tier (the way in, from the Tiers).
  2. The walkway under the line: the scavengers' shelf and lamp [PROPOSED].
  3. The line and its offerings, seen and never taken.
  4. The white streets, crossed shadow to shadow.
  5. The noble houses, Sancterre and Valmont-Lune [PROPOSED names], with gilt domes gone white.
  6. The Unison's street and plaza. Be silent, keep low, keep your voice in the Narrow. The white house you climb to hide.
  7. The Glassmarket.
- *Wonder:* the Glassmarket and its Bleached.
- *Keeper:* [PROPOSED] a half-white scavenger, the **Tallyman** (a Tiers nickname, like the Tiers' own plain names; no collision in story/), who goes up every night to count the Glassmarket's people. They are thinning, like the gull (B2P2:581-583). Reach him across the widest gap and he gives the set and his one line. No fight.
- *Shortcut:* [PROPOSED] the scavengers' drop-line from a noble house's parapet down to the shelf, let down from above.
- *Inventions:* the shelf, the lamp, the two names, the Tallyman, the drop-line, the gear set.

**(b) As (a), with the Mirror Walks kept as a dead ruin.** The arrays have gone shell-white, as the Eye has where nobody
polishes it (B2P2:2399). They throw no beam, and there is no Polisher. I cannot show from the text that the book allows
this. The ordinance "within the chains" (B2P2:471) is posted in the Tiers, which exist only since the Fracture, so it could
be new. But "had been the only mirror the Altar permitted in all the Heights" (B2P2:2399) reads as true before the Fracture
too, and that leaves no room for two thousand polishers (V2:87). This design needs you to rule that the Walks predate the
ordinance.

**(c) No keeper.** A place you cross and come back from: rooms 1 to 7 of (a). The gear set lies in a noble house's chest,
and the drop-line is the way home. The only invention is the shelf, the lamp and the line.

**Recommendation: (a).** It is built only from the book's own places (the gutted house, the line, the white streets, the
Glassmarket, the Unison's street). It keeps the shape every great place has (lamp, keeper, wonder, shortcut, set). Its
inventions are small, and none runs against a quoted line. (b) rests on a reading the text leans against. (c) is safe but
gives the Heights' strongest place no one to meet.

## 4. What must never be done there (Ashfall 419, Book Two)

- Meet, find or speak with Solenne. Disturb the nest, the offerings or the drawings.
- Meet the lone Wired woman, or learn where Solenne is ("She is on the sixth chain", B2P2:1187-1235).
- Stop, break, scatter or kill the Unison, or ring the city or a chain (B2P2:2113-2165).
- Move the line, by any amount (B2P2:2209; 2535-2541).
- Go through the iron door or the Lightward Nave (B2P2:1407).
- Touch the Bleached. Show the child on the woman's hip (B2P2:1021; V3b:13).
- Reach the Foundry or the sixth anchor-house.

## 5. A built line the book contradicts

`js/heights92.js:157`, the black-market woman's topic "The mirror":

> The only one in the Tiers, and it stays under the cloth. Mirrors are illegal. Mirrors are what the Bleach shines from, up on the Walks. You want it, you buy it covered, and you never take the cloth off down here.

Corrected, in the book's own words (B2P2:471, 665):

> The only one in the Tiers, and it stays under the cloth. Mirrors are illegal. A reflection is a second light, and the Light is jealous. You want it, you buy it covered, and you never take the cloth off down here.

French: *Le seul des Étages, et il reste sous le tissu. Les miroirs sont illégaux. Un reflet est une seconde lumière, et la
Lumière est jalouse. Vous le voulez, vous l'achetez couvert, et vous n'enlevez jamais le tissu ici.*

The same wrong line is also in `tools/i18n/p92-heights.tsv:81`, in the Phase 92 record (line 166), and in Phase 89's
reconciliation (line 206). None of them is edited here.
