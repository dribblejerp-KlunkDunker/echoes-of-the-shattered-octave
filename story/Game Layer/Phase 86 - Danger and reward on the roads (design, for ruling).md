# Phase 86: danger and reward on the roads (design, for the owner's ruling)

From the roadmap: "Danger and reward on the roads: what walks each road by region, hour and weather; camps; named keepers; bounties given by the people already on the roads. Done when the roads are not safe in the same way twice." The world plan (approved 13 Nov) already gives each road its danger: briar-sprites, mire-drones and hounds on the Flume road; mire-drones in the Sunk Lanes, worse in storm; a quiet first stretch on the drove road and "hounds den in the Hollows after dark". This design keeps all of that and makes it move. Everything below that is not already in the game is **[PROPOSED]**.

## What the game has now

- **Every road's foes are placed once and never change.** The Greenroot road has 2 hounds on the Long Common and a Dirge, swarms and hounds in Greenroot Wood; the Stride has 9 briar-sprites; the Pound Stair 2 mire-drones and 2 hounds; the Grey Wethers and the Hundred Hollows have nothing at all. Day or night, rain or fine, it is the same.
- **The only thing that moves is the ambush roll on a map journey** (Phase 84: one roll for each stretch, higher at night, in bad weather and on dangerous ground). It draws from a short list of foes that knows only five places.
- **Bounties:** one, the Writ of Clearing on Oakhaven's board ("clear 4 beasts off the roads", 35 sovereigns). None of the people met on the roads gives one.
- **The weather has six states** (clear, overcast, rain, storm, mist, snow), and the day has dawn, day, dusk and night. Nothing on the roads listens to either yet, except the Phase 83 and 85 places built to.

## The design

### 1. What walks each road, by hour and weather [PROPOSED]

Each road gets a **roster**: who is there by day, who comes out at dusk and night, and who comes with rain, storm, mist or snow. When you step onto a stretch, the game reads the hour and the weather and puts out that stretch's company at fixed places along it (beside the road, in the verge, at the fords, never on top of a finger-post or a person). Leave and come back in a different hour or weather and it is a different road.

| Road | By day | Dusk and night adds | Rain / storm adds | Mist adds | Snow |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Greenroot road** (the Long Common, the middle, Greenroot Wood) | a pair of hounds on the Common; swarms in the wood | the pack: 3 more hounds, and a Dirge out of the Wood | hounds come closer to the road ("noise brings the hounds", and rain hides your noise and theirs) | hush-moths on the Wood stretch | the road is quiet: half the hounds |
| **Flume road** (the Stride, the Pound Stair) | briar-sprites in the verges | mire-drones come up out of the pounds onto the banks | after rain the briar wakes: twice the sprites; in storm the pounds surge and drones are out all day | nothing new; you see less | the briar sleeps: no sprites |
| **Drove road** (the Grey Wethers, the Hundred Hollows) | the Wethers are quiet, as the plan says | hounds den in the Hollows after dark, as the plan says; Quiet Faith brothers keep a vigil among the stones | nothing new | the Hollows' hounds come out by day in mist | woolwights come down off the high ground |
| **Sunk Lanes and Cross Pool** | drones at the pools' edges | (as built) | (as built: storm brings two more and danger 2) | — | — |
| **The Office column** (see 4) | walks one road a day | camps at night | holds up in its camp in storm | — | — |

**Not chance:** which places get which foes on a given visit is worked out from the day, the hour and the weather, like the Deep Grove's ways: the same for every player on the same day, and the same for the tests. *(Question 1.)*

The **finger-posts and notice boards tell you the road's mood**: the board at each end carries one line on what is out today ("Hounds on the Common since Hearthday. Keep your voice down."), so a careful player can read the road before walking it.

The **map-journey ambush** (Phase 84) draws from the same rosters, so what jumps you on the way is what walks that stretch at that hour.

### 2. Camps [PROPOSED]

Two kinds, hostile and friendly, a few on each road.

**Hostile camps** (a tent or a ring of stones, a fire or the lack of one, three or four foes, and a crate):
- **An Office survey camp** on the Greenroot road's middle stretch, by the toll-house: two proctors and an outrider round a table with a theodolite on it. On the table, a ledger of names taken on the road, which you can read.
- **A Quiet Faith vigil** among the Grey Wethers at night: tongueless brothers round a fire laid but never lit. Silent; they make no sound when they run.
- **The hound den** in the Hundred Hollows (canon, from the plan), with what the hounds have dragged in.
- **A briar-bed** on the Stride's verge: a knot of sprites round something they have stripped.

A camp is **occupied for some days, then cold for some days**, on a cycle worked out from the day. **Clear one and it stays cold for an eight-day week.** A cold camp still has its ashes and its marks, and sometimes something left behind.

**Friendly camps** (rest, nothing to fight):
- **The raft-hands' fire** on the Stride, where the two walking home stop for the night.
- **The drovers' halt at the Three Pines** (already the Phase 84 night halt).

At a friendly camp you can **sit by the fire until morning** (the hours pass, your health comes back, and the night's roster goes by you). It is the walking version of the Phase 84 night halts.

### 3. Named keepers on the roads [PROPOSED]

One for each road. Each has its own hour and weather, so you have to go out at the right time to find it, and its own bounty (see 4). Each is a creature of a kind already in the game grown large, as the big places' keepers are ("growth has lost its stop").

| Road | Keeper [PROPOSED name] | What it is | When and where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greenroot road | **the Hiss** | the pack's mother, a Cacophony Hound grown to the size of a pony; you hear her before you see her | the Long Common, at night in rain |
| Flume road | **the Briar Queen** | a briar-sprite colony grown into one body the size of a cart, every thorn with its little lamp | the Stride's verges, the day after rain |
| Drove road | **the Hollow Dog** | the hound that dens deepest in the Hollows | the Hundred Hollows, in mist, day or night |
| Sunk Lanes | **the Sunk Bell** | a mire-drone grown under a fallen bell-tower; its note is the bell's | the Lanes' deepest pool, in storm |

Each keeper, put down, **stays down for a week**, then something else takes its place (the same name, a little different). It drops a **trophy** (the Hiss's collar of static, the Queen's lamps, the Dog's den-bone, the Bell's clapper) and coin. *(Question 4: whether trophies are only for selling and bounties, or gear.)*

### 4. Bounties from the people already on the roads [PROPOSED]

Every road already has someone to meet. Each of them now has **one keeper bounty** (once) and **one week's work** (repeatable, renewed every eight-day week).

| Who | Where | Keeper bounty | The week's work |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The turf-cutter** | the Long Common | the Hiss: "She had my dog. Bring me the collar." | quiet the Common: 6 hounds |
| **The lengthsman** | the Pound Stair | the Briar Queen: "She's had the bootlaces off every raft-hand on the Stride." | clear the briar off the lock-ladders: 8 sprites |
| **The returners** (the family with the handcart) | the drove road | the Hollow Dog: "It sits on the way to our farm." | see them safe: walk with them from the Wethers to the Hollows' end |
| **The salvagers** | Cross Pool | the Sunk Bell: "It's on our best roof." | lead off the garret roofs (bring 4 pieces) |

Payment is **sovereigns and the giver's good word**. A keeper bounty pays about what a big place's strongbox holds; the week's work pays about what Oakhaven's Writ of Clearing pays (35 sovereigns). The boards carry each bounty too, under "To take".

### 4b. The Office column [PROPOSED]

The one thing that moves from road to road: **an Office assessor and her column** (two proctors, an outrider, a hushwarden) walking the Reach's roads to register who is on them. One road a day, in turn; they camp at night by the road; in storm they stay in their camp. You can fight them, or keep out of their way (the column is slow and loud, and the board says which road they are on). Nobody on the roads gives a bounty on them; whether fighting them goes in a book is part of question 5. *(Question 5.)*

## What is not changing

The Kestrel still carries you between countries. The big places (the Howe, the Dry Flume, the Antler Galleries, the Deep Grove) keep the foes they were built with; this phase is about the roads. The Deep Grove stays the one place where nothing attacks. The danger numbers on the chart stay as they are; the new road news goes on the boards, not the chart. A save from before this phase loads with every camp occupied, every keeper alive, and no bounty taken.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Rosters worked out from the day, hour and weather, never chance**, so the same day's road is the same for everyone and the tests can rely on it. *Recommend: yes.* (The alternative is a fresh roll each time you step onto a stretch.)
2. **The rosters as in the table above:** packs at night, the briar waking after rain, drones out in storm, the Hollows' hounds out in mist, the roads quieter in snow. *Recommend: yes.*
3. **Camps:** hostile camps on a cycle, cold for a week once cleared; friendly fires where you can sit until morning. *Recommend: yes.*
4. **Keepers' trophies: only for selling and bounties, not new gear.** You asked to keep the new gear to four in Phase 85, so I would not add more here. *Recommend: trophies only.*
5. **The Office column, walking a road a day.** *Recommend: yes; it is the one danger that comes looking for you, and it fits the books.* And: should fighting it count as a crime with the Office? *Recommend: yes, a small one, so keeping out of its way is worth something.*
6. **Names:** the Hiss, the Briar Queen, the Hollow Dog, the Sunk Bell, the bounty lines. *Recommend: keep, or give your own.*
7. **Order of building:** the rosters and the board lines first, then camps, then keepers and bounties, then the column. *Recommend: this order.*
