# Phase 64 — ROADMAP R10 wave 3: Port Cantabile's Yard and Mole keep the hours

*Recorded 8 Nov 2026 from the game's translation file (`tools/i18n/p64-cantabile.tsv`). The people are [PROPOSED] (roles, no names). The harbour, its trades and its customs are canon (World Bible Vol IV, Book Two).*

## What is new, in short

- **Twenty-one people** on the Lantern Yard and the Long Mole of the "A trip to Port Cantabile" extra (`js/life64-cantabile.js`), built on the canon trades (salvage, pilotage, vox-bottles) and customs (the Articles, the Mole-bell, Hesper's houses):
  - by day: net-menders, a fishwife, two salvage divers, a rope-coiler, Hesper's children and her eldest, a pilot, two Free Captains, a boy sluicing the Mole, a caulker, a sail-mender and a water-carrier;
  - at noon: the Articles are read;
  - at dusk and after: the lamplighter, the night watch on the Mole-head, and a man fishing off the Mole.
- **They keep to the quay.** Nobody walks into the water or up the cliff. The grown-ups go home up the Salt Stair; the children go in at Hesper's doors.
- **When the Mole-bell rings they stand still and say nothing** until the harbour has counted.
- **Driftwood Cay is left as it is,** a quiet burying-ground.

## Questions for sleeves
