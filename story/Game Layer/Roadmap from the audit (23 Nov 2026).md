# Roadmap from the audit (23 November 2026)

The owner, 23 Nov, 6:24 pm: "the game audit. put a road map together. I want to start seeing more added to the game visually as well".

This is the audit's to-do list (`Audit of 23 Nov 2026 - the game against the books, the quests, the dialogue, with ratings.md`, section 8) put into phases and an order, with something new to look at in every step. It adds to the Fable 5.2 roadmap of 21 Nov and does not replace it: Phases 114, 115 and 117 to 120 keep their numbers and are placed below.

## How it is ordered

1. **Every step pairs something to see with something to play.** Two builders work at a time (three exhausted the usage limit), so each step is two phases side by side, and at least one of the two puts new pictures on the screen. Pictures are sent as each lands.
2. **The root first.** Who the traveller is comes before the callings, and the callings before the scenes and quests that lean on them.
3. **The books before every design.** Each phase begins with a lore brief quoting file and line. Everything invented is marked [PROPOSED]. The owner has accepted proposals in advance "so long as it makes sense"; anything a book contradicts, anything that cannot be undone, and anything of doubtful sense still comes to him first.
4. **The standing rules hold:** all art made in code; nothing visual uses random numbers; every new look has an Options switch and is off in the Original preset; French for every new line; a failing test first; every phase recorded here, in Vol VIII, the README and the Project.
