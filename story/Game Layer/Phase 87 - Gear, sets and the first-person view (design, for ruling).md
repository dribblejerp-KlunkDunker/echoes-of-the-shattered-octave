# Phase 87: gear, matching sets and the first-person view (design, for the owner's ruling)

The owner, 14 Nov: "lets add several new gloves and shields. I want to add in capes as well. There should be more ranged weapons as well. Each item should look unique and interesting. more helmets or at least head pieces even bandanas and caps. For each weapon or worn item there should also be a full matching set as well. Yes add bonuses for having a matching set with smaller bonuses for at least 3 matching items. the first person view needs work also so that the hands and weapons look better ... The glass arm is also far too bright." With a reference picture: a first-person view on a harbour quay, a broad steel sword held upright in a leather-gloved right hand, a faceted azure glass left arm, a stone-and-brass HUD.

Done already (14 Nov), before this design: the Old Cantor's Staff, the Punt-Pole and the Lengthsman's Hook are two-handed; the glass arm is deeper azure, keeps its painted facets, and gives off about a quarter of the light it did.

Every set name, piece name, number and bonus below is **[PROPOSED]**. Factions and places are the books' and the bibles'.

## What the game has now

- **27 weapons** (18 one-handed, 6 two-handed, 3 ranged), **26 armour pieces** in five slots: head 5, body 10, hands 3, feet 5, shield 3. No capes, no sets, no set bonuses.
- **The first-person picture** is eleven shared paintings from the first week of the build (sword, longsword, knife, axe, mace, maul, spear, pistol, crossbow, bare hands, the glass arm). Every newer weapon is one of those eleven, re-coloured. The right hand is drawn small and low, the blade thin, and the light on it does not follow the scene.

## The design

### 1. A sixth slot: the cape [PROPOSED]

A **cape** slot (cape, cloak, plaid, mantle or pennon) beside head, body, hands, feet and shield. A cape gives a little armour and a property of its own (warmth in snow, quiet, rain off). It shows on the character card, on the co-op partner and ghost, and in first person as a corner of cloth that swings into the bottom of the view when you turn hard or run. Capes are made in code like everything else: cloth that hangs, with the hem and the clasp drawn per cape.

### 2. Fourteen matching sets [PROPOSED]

Every existing weapon and worn piece goes into a set, and every set is filled out to a **full set**: head, body, hands, feet, cape, and its weapon(s), with a shield where the set's weapon is one-handed. New pieces (marked **+**) fill the gaps. That is **58 new items**: 14 capes, 9 head pieces, 11 gloves, 9 boots, 5 body pieces, 4 shields and 6 ranged weapons.

### 3. Set bonuses [PROPOSED]

- **Three pieces of one set** (any three, weapon included): a small bonus.
- **The full set** (every piece the set lists, shield only if the set has one; for sets with several weapons, any one of them): a larger bonus, on top.
- Bonuses are about how the set's people live, not plain numbers only. Examples:

| Set | 3 pieces | Full set |
|---|---|---|
| Tanner's Row | +1 armour | fatigue comes back 20% faster |
| Hedge-weavers' Hush | footsteps 20% quieter | footsteps silent on soft ground; enemies hear you at half the distance |
| Synod Arsenal | +2 armour | blocks with the kite shield cost no fatigue |
| Carillon Legion | +2 armour | hits that land on you ring: the Cacophony near you takes 3 damage |
| The Office's Proctors | Proctors at a distance take you for one of their own (as the clerk's robes do now) | the Office column passes you by |
| The Cantors | Songs cost 10% less voice | Songs last a third longer |
| The Free Captains | swings 10% faster | ranged weapons reload a third faster; no fall damage from a ship's height |
| The Drovers | fatigue 15% slower on the roads | the pony rides 10% faster; hounds come at you one at a time |
| The Old Reach | iron-bough and flint hit 15% harder | the Deep Grove's ways are always the low note |

All fourteen are in the build list; each gets one line in the item's description, and the pack and the character card show "Tanner's Row: 3 of 8".

### 4. More ranged weapons [PROPOSED]

Six new, so ranged goes from 3 to 9: **Felted Sling** (stones; the quietest ranged weapon), **Shepherd's Sling** (stones; longer reach), **Office Carbine** (the Office marines' gun in Book Three: glass shot, two hands, loud), **Works Hand-Crossbow** (one hand, short bolts), **Harpoon Gun** (a corsair's; pulls what it hits towards you, like the Lengthsman's Hook at range), **Hornbow** (antler and sinew; arrows; you cannot shoot it from the saddle, as Phase 84 already says of bows). New ammunition: stones (picked up anywhere outdoors), arrows, short bolts, harpoons (recovered).

### 5. Every item looks like itself [PROPOSED]

Each new piece gets: a pixel icon of its own in the pack; its own entry in the gear-look table (shape, material, colours, pattern), so the character card, the co-op partner and the first-person arms all draw it as itself; and a line in the description saying which set it belongs to. No two pieces share a look, which the tests check (they already do for weapons and armour).

### 6. The first-person view, to the owner's picture [PROPOSED]

- **New first-person paintings, made in code, one per weapon, at twice the present detail.** Not eleven shared pictures re-coloured: each weapon's picture is built from its gear-look entry (kind, length, materials, colours), so a new weapon gets its own picture without new art.
- **The right hand and weapon, as in the picture:** bigger and nearer, the blade held more upright, a broad blade with a bevel, a fuller, an edge highlight and a shadowed side; a cross-guard and a wrapped grip; the glove you wear, with its own seams and cuff, closing round the grip.
- **Lit by the scene:** the light on the blade and glove follows the time of day, the sky and nearby lamps and fires (warm by a fire, cold under an overcast sky, dark at night), as the world already is.
- **The glass arm** as in the picture: azure facets with dark seams and a few bright edges (done; question 6 asks if it is right now).
- **Movement:** a slight bob while walking, the swing and the thrust re-timed to the bigger blade, the shield and the cape corner coming in at the edges.

### 7. Where the new things are found [PROPOSED]

Each set is found in its home: sold by the town's shops there (the cheaper pieces), carried by the people there (dropped by its fighters), and the best one or two pieces of each set are in that region's big place or given for a task. So a full set means travelling and exploring, not one shop.

## What is not changing

The stats of the existing 53 items stay as they are, except the three made two-handed today. Saves from before this phase load with an empty cape slot. Nothing is taken off the player.

2. **The fourteen sets as listed,** their names, and which existing item goes in which. *Recommend: as listed; rename or move any.*

4. **The six new ranged weapons** (two slings, the Office Carbine, a hand-crossbow, a harpoon gun, a hornbow) and their ammunition. *Recommend: yes.*
5. **The first-person paintings rebuilt in code, one per weapon, to your picture.** *Recommend: yes, and first in the build, so every new weapon gets its picture as it is made.*

7. **Order of building:** (a) the first-person paintings and hands; (b) the cape slot and the set system with bonuses; (c) the new pieces, one set at a time, with pictures of each set as it is finished; (d) the ranged weapons and their ammunition; (e) where everything is found. *Recommend: this order.*

"1 yes, 2 is a yes, yes, yes, 5 is a yes, the glass arm does look better yes, 7 isa yes". All seven approved as recommended: the cape slot; the fourteen sets as listed; bonuses at three pieces and the full set; the six ranged weapons; the first-person paintings rebuilt in code, first; the glass arm kept as it is now; the order of building (a) first-person view, (b) capes and the set system, (c) the new pieces set by set, (d) ranged weapons and ammunition, (e) where everything is found.
