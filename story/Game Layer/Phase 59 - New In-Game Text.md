# Phase 59 — Release road R5 #7: the Reedside Conservatory and reed orchard

*Recorded 7 Nov 2026 from the game's translation file (`tools/i18n/p59-conservatory.tsv`). Lines marked [PROPOSED] are invention not yet ruled on.*

## What is new

A door in the east wall of the Conservatory Reading Room (Ilse Brennan's library, `js/libraries26.js`) opens on the rest of the school (`js/conservatory59.js`), in Parts One and Two:

- **The reed-hall** (Book Four Part One ch. 4): high dark beams, a fire of dry wind-reed stalks, the kitchen; pews in rows facing the slate board.
- **The slate board**: Ilse's line from the first morning she came back to teach in 408 (Book Four).
- **The reed-rack over the door**: four hundred pegs, forty reeds; the head of school will not replace the dead students' reeds (Vol II).
- **Ilse's desk**: from Dawnday, three close-written pages, the first word "Lyra," and the rest face down. Book Four: she wrote the letter "in Harvestmonth, the week the Church came". Nothing of it is shown.
- **The reed orchard**, against the east Hedge: rows of wind-reed, the oldest tree's hollow that Cantors hum into on the Longest Night, the tally-board of nights kept (Vol V), and a flagstone heaved by an iron-bark's roots where the Hedge meets the orchard wall. The old stair stays sealed: nothing there opens.
- **Also fixed**: the Moot-house library's benches used a sprite that does not exist and were invisible; they are pews now.

## Questions for sleeves

1. That Ilse's 408 line is still on the slate board, never wiped.
2. The box of old exercise slates, with L. Ashgrove's: "The tending note." / "Again. Lower. Slower. Good."
3. The youngest student (unnamed, eight), the tending note held together, and her line "It took Cantor Ashgrove till she was nine." (Book Four says Lyra learned it at eight; this is a joke the students tell.)
4. Humming into the hollow: the note "goes down, further than any tree is deep, and does not come back." It hints at the Root without saying so.
5. The heaved flagstone that rings: a hint of the old stair, with no slab and no ring showing (`js/longnight17.js` shows the lifted slab after Book One).
