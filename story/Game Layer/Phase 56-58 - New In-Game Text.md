## What is new, in short

- **Cargo night (R9 item 14)** (`js/cargo56.js`). Book One ch. 8 on the Kestrel's mast-ladder: Stall the Clerk on Driftday night, the boot, the count; the third crate at the dead hour, WG-41,004, the engineer's slate, "Annet. You're going home."; the long gun over the slag-slope.
- **Tobiah's arc (R9 item 18)** (`js/tobiah57.js`). Book One ch. 5: K. Vance's book, by calling; the Stage Nine kit after the Church comes.
- **The eight men at the pumps (R9 item 19)** (`js/pumps58.js`). Book One ch. 19: the Arbiter's voice in the Malting's archive, and the carriage page.

## Questions for sleeves

Everything below is [PROPOSED] and waits for your ruling.

1. Stall the Clerk as play: three rounds, each with two safe topics (the book's: the wind, the Reach, an invented Cantabile tariff, the price of drift-glass) and one that draws his eye (the crates, the sail-locker, the warrant). Twice wrong and Vane takes over, as in the book. Picking the boot up makes him note it; nothing comes of that yet.
2. That you, not Vane, may tell Annet she is going home.
3. That you carry the long gun's lock and shot to the slag-slope at Depot Three (R6's "Vane's long gun in the quarry mud" can find it there later).
4. Tobiah by calling: a Blade is mistaken for a man who could sign K. Vance's book ("Did you know him? At the Conservatory?"); an archivist is asked whether the Office keeps everything ("Nobody files a margin.").
5. The Arbiter's clue reaches a non-glassed player through Kaelen's arm. A pencil tick beside the names you said aloud at the Rite.
