## Lore notes [PROPOSED]
- Sermons are Dawnday (first light), Stasisday (the hour named once at dusk) and Memoryday (the names, evening). They end at the Greening: the Synod does not go to Whisper-Grove (the Drowned Lanterns canon), so there is no congregation there to preach to; one closing logbook line is written for a player who ever met a sermon.
- 'The dead are being echoed' is the Church's sermon refrain on all three days. Since the Grove went quiet the Echo no longer catches the dead of its own accord [PROPOSED], so the Church 'catches' them (the names sermon).
- Eclipses: a new-moon Dawnday (calendar11's moon) that a day hash picks, 18 a canonical year; never a feast, festival or story day; never during the Rite or the escape timer. Folk belief: the dogs know first and the Hush keeps closest at a dark noon.

## Sermon: first light (Dawnday, 06:30-09:00): title, five lines, summary for Reduce motion, closing [PROPOSED]
- First light
- Friends. We are met at first light, as the Church has always met, because it is the one hour that never needed asking for.
- There were a thousand bells once, and a thousand ears, and each ear heard a different morning. Now there is one Note. One Note, one town, held. Hold it and you are held.
- You have felt the quiet at the edge of the wood. Do not call it a hunger. A quiet is only a note that has not been asked to sound. We mean to ask it. All of us, together.
- The dead are being echoed. Every name that went silent in the Reach is a note some mouth is still holding, and it is not yet ours. Sing in unison and they are carried. Sing alone and you drop them.
- Go gently, and in tune. Say nothing under the Note. That is not a rule. It is how a held thing is held.
- The deacon preaches the Pure Note: one note, one town, held together at first light. The quiet is only a note not yet asked to sound, and the dead are being echoed, he says, so long as nobody sings alone.
- The congregation answers in one breath: Held. You are a half-beat late. Nobody turns round.
