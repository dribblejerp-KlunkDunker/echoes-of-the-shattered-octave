# Lore from B1, B2 (three files) and B4 (two files): grep-first pass, 22 Nov 2026

Short names: B1 = Book One full manuscript; B2P1a = Part One Ch1-3; B2P1b = Part One Ch4-7; B2P2 = Part Two Ch8-14; B4P1 = Book Four Part One; B4P2 = Book Four Part Two.
Method: grep -n over all six files for every word in the brief plus extras (Null, Sentry, Legion, wall-walk, basket, Bastion, Greer, Ward, causeway, render, Disposal, Maw, Edge). Every real hit was read in context. Most hits for Rift, Hollow, rota, Throat, Shelf, Mote, tipped, carts were false (drift, throat, shelf of books, etc.) and are not logged.
Dating note: B1 is Harvestmonth (the book itself places Oakhaven's quarry pumps "in Harvestmonth", B4P1:946). B2 starts 4 Ashfall (B2P1a:218). Book Four runs Frostmonth (B4P1:12, 16th-18th Frostmonth), AFTER Book Three. Year 419 is inferred, not printed in the lines I read.

## The wall and the Watch
B4P1:12 "Null-Watch, and Bastion Fortis. Driftday the sixteenth of Frostmonth, to Nullday the eighteenth." Chapter opens at Null-Watch. LATER (Frostmonth, after Book Three).
B4P1:18 "He stood on the wall of Null-Watch in the first snow of the winter, with the sack on his arm" Kaelen is on the wall; first snow of winter has fallen. LATER.
B4P1:64 "the wall-walk between Towers Five and Six, where the Watch had kept him the first week, with the voiceless wind coming over the obsidian off the edge of the world." There is a wall-walk between Towers Five and Six, the wall is obsidian, wind off the edge is "voiceless". LATER.
B4P1:64 "Two-Eleven had gone a little way off along the wall to talk about his dog to a battery crew" Trooper Two-Eleven has a dog and talks to battery crews; batteries on the wall. LATER (he is also described as talking to Vane, see B4P1:120).
B4P1:120 "He had watched Two-Eleven do it at the parapet for Vane. Laying a blanket of talk over a thing so that the thing would not have to be looked at." The Watch's talking is done on purpose, to cover a grief; Two-Eleven did it for Vane at the parapet. LATER, and refers to events inside Book Three.
B4P1:106 "walked away along the wall-walk toward the stair and the rope and the *Kestrel*" The stair and the rope lead down from the wall-walk; the Kestrel was moored at a Watch mast. LATER.
B4P1:134 "It burned when I took the hawser at the Watch" Kaelen took a hawser at the Watch (Book Three event, not described here). LATER.
B4P1:680 "the trooper called Sixty at the valves. Sixty was a lean silent man from the Watch with a face like a closed door, who had asked to come" A Watch trooper called Sixty left the Watch to join the Kestrel. LATER (after Book Three).
B4P1:912 "It was the ugliest thing Vane had ever seen. He had seen the walls of Null-Watch." Vane's measure of ugliness is the Watch walls. LATER.
B4P2:36 "come down from the Heights to the Flume crossing on a chime out of Null-Watch, eight days before" The Watch sends messages by chime (a signalling method) to ships. LATER.
B4P1:192 "She said it in iron-cant, through a boy who reads it for her now" "She" is the General (B4P1:182 "The General said you'd come"); she speaks iron-cant and now needs a boy to read it for her. The General is not named in these lines. LATER.
B4P1:182 "We had a chime from the Watch. The General said you'd come, and why." The General at the Watch knew in advance that Kaelen was going to Fortis. LATER.
B2P1b:1217 "which he had won off a Null-Watch quartermaster at cards in his first year as a captain" Vane's gun Last Rites, Fortis-made, was won from a Null-Watch quartermaster years ago. Not named here, so it cannot be shown to be Greer. BEFORE 419 (years before).
B2P1a:288 "I'll fly the Null Sentry line for you with the Legion shooting at me" A "Null Sentry line" is a known run, dangerous with the Legion shooting. Ashfall 419, early (4 Ashfall).
B1:5286 "— *A Plain Account of the Cacophony*, Null-Watch Press" There is a Null-Watch Press that prints a tract on the Cacophony (the hounds). Epigraph only. Before / Harvestmonth.

## The rope and the basket
B4P1:106 see above: "the stair and the rope and the *Kestrel*". The rope is climbed up to the wall ("She came up the rope to say goodbye", B4P1:62). Lyra goes up the rope in snow, at the first hour. LATER.
Nothing about a crane, buttress, knots, notes, or the Quartermaster's bread in these files. Baskets that turn up are unrelated (Oakhaven basket-maker Tam; Sol-Luminary cradles "great iron baskets hung on chains", B2P2:621 and B4P2:132; the plaza woman's shopping basket, B4P1:1164).

## The Shelf
The Shelf is mentioned only as a place behind the Book Three events. Everything is LATER.
B4P1:36 "They had planned the parting on the Shelf, the night after the last card, while he sat on the cutter four miles off with the sack on his arm" The Shelf has a fire and cards ("the last card"); Kaelen stayed on the cutter FOUR MILES off. 
B4P1:54 "looked at him across the Shelf's fire" There is a fire on the Shelf.
B4P1:280 "the word Lyra had used for the Shelf" Lyra's word for the Shelf is "quiet"; the quiet goes through floors as a line, soft, no sound.
B4P1:946 "the way the man on the Shelf had moved his nine stones, the way old Nan had twisted her hands" A Hollow on the Shelf moves nine stones over and over; "old Nan" twists her hands.
B4P1:952 "Thinner than anyone on the Shelf." Shelf Hollows are thin but less so than the Site Nine pump-hands.
B4P1:1252 "She had told Tessa Wray so on the Shelf." Tessa Wray is on the Shelf; Sable spoke to her.
B4P1:1544 "She had read the names of the forty-seven on the Shelf aloud at the fire" Sable read 47 names aloud at the Shelf fire. (47 may be the dead, or the names; the line does not say.)
B4P1:1911 "the slate in Kaelen's hand he had handed back on the Shelf that said *GOOD*" Plans are passed on slates; Kaelen wrote GOOD on the Shelf.
B4P1:2077 "the night on the Shelf when they had all said the date aloud" The company stood on the Shelf and said the date of the Longest Night, 30 Frostmonth.
B4P1:2097 "Kaelen had said it on the Shelf, Tobiah told her, alone on the cutter, to the glass." Kaelen on the cutter.
B4P2:882 "on the *Kestrel*, on the Shelf, on the *Equity*, in the reed-hall at Reedside" Sable's sleepless listening lists the Shelf as a stop between the Kestrel and the Equity.
B4P2:1841 "the face she had seen on the Shelf on people walking into the quiet. On Bridie Cole in the chair." People on the Shelf walk into the quiet and the face goes "soft", an ease with the colour going out. Bridie Cole sat in a chair (a death or a Rendering, unclear). LATER.
No Rendered / Rendering words appear in these files. No doorways, holes, ends, Lip, two tins or under-Shelf in these files.

## Redoubt Zero, the Maw and the Edge
B1:3888 "Whisper-Grove to the garrison at Redoubt Zero. Thirteen hundred-odd crates' worth, and I signed for every one" Redoubt Zero is a garrison that receives Whisper-Grove cores (people) by Vane's skyship; about 1,300 crates over two years. Said in Harvestmonth (before Book Three). This is the only mention of Redoubt Zero in my files. It is a GARRISON receiving cores by crate, delivered by air. It is not described otherwise; no causeway or rotation road here.
B4P1:926 "Straining, the way it had strained toward the edge of the world on the wall of Null-Watch." Mote leaned out toward "the edge of the world" while on the wall of Null-Watch. LATER (Book Three time, recalled).
B4P1:64 "the voiceless wind coming over the obsidian off the edge of the world" the Edge is beyond the wall; wind from it makes no voice. LATER.
Nothing about the Maw.

## Hazards and creatures
B2P1a:145 "a thing out of the Rift ... The shot went in and was gone. The body closed over the hole like water over a dropped stone." The only use of the word Rift in these files: Oona Salt shoots "a thing out of the Rift" (the quiet on the Bitter Nancy) with 19 sovereigns of Aether-Glass and nothing happens. Iron, cold water and calm people work against it (B2P1a:147). Ashfall 419 early.
B2P1a:141 "The quiet took him across the thighs. It did not lunge; it *leaned* ... absolutely nothing coming out of it" The quiet leans, takes a man to the ground without a sound; people nearby do not know he fell. Ashfall 419 early.
B4P1:264-280 "the stone made no sound at all, and the rod went on ... On one side of it the stone was stone ... On the other side it looked exactly the same, and it was not there." The Fortis quiet is a line that climbs stone faster than a man can walk backward; running is heavy and heavy goes through; step backward in the same footprints. Sounders' rods are tapped to test. LATER (Frostmonth 18).
B4P1:284-286 victims sink "like going down into sleep" and the face "went soft", relieved. Ulf Garrow alone did not go soft. LATER.
B2P1b:1551 Mote: "pale ... a soft folded shape ... fringe along its underside ... faintly lit from inside ... violet". Mote "leans", warm as bath-water, eats a person's worst memory or fear (B2P1b:1577, B4P1:26 "Mote took the fear. 28 Ashfall."). It silences the hold's whispering cores (B2P2:112-116). Mote is Vesper's; Vesper was five years at Site Nine's pipe (B4P1:932). Ashfall 419.
B4P1:926-952 Site Nine pipe "Black iron, as wide as a house ... into the water at the very lip of the trench"; it keeps drawing by itself after Hollows are removed (B4P1:1050). LATER.
Nothing on gravewells, Throat-Wraiths, the Null-Amalgam, the Unrotting, insects or birds at the Rift. B1:2271 "a jitter in the air along the forest's edge" is the hounds (Cacophony) at Oakhaven, not the Rift.

## Contradictions
- B4P1:36 says the cutter was four miles off the Shelf "the night after the last card"; B4P1:54 has Kaelen across the Shelf's fire from Tobiah. He could not do both on the same night unless the fire scene was earlier. Mild; may be two nights.
- B4P1:134 "burned ... when I took the hawser at the Watch" vs B4P1:106 the Kestrel at the Watch mast: consistent.
- B4P1:1252 says Sable told Tessa Wray "so on the Shelf"; B4P1:1544 "forty-seven on the Shelf aloud": Sable was on the Shelf in Book Three; consistent.
- No contradiction with other volumes that I can see from these files alone.
