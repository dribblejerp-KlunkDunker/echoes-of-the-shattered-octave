## Contradictions inside this file or with other volumes

1. Counts of the Shelf: "three hundred and seventy-nine" (lines 75, 117), "three hundred" (line 359), "three hundred and sixty-five people on a shelf" (line 665), "three hundred and ninety-six cores" for the cargo (lines 657, 1541). Line 665 says 365 people on the Shelf, which does not match 379. 365 is also the number of cracked cores in bin seven (line 655), and 131 + 232 = 363. Treat 379 (before the Shelf learns of the Hall) as the best figure for 23 Ashfall, but flag it.
2. The Malting residuals: 200 sent east (line 773) but only 22 + 8 + 14 = 44 accounted for. The file does not say what happened to the other 156. Do not invent it.
3. Knots vs notes: 2,022 knots (line 75) against Fen's "nearly two thousand notes" (line 365). Six years of nightly baskets is about 2,190 nights, so 2,022 is short by about 170. The file does not explain the gap. The gap is consistent with baskets not going down on every night, and the file does not say.
4. The Quartermaster's age: "He was sixty" (line 109). He has been quartermaster since 406 (line 289) and was condemned in 413 (line 347). That is consistent.
5. The Quartermaster is an old garrison man from before the crack (line 347), yet line 289 says "Penal legion from the spring of 413". The file treats the old garrison and the penal legion as one place with a changeover in 413.
6. Fen is "F-four" and has her docket in bin seven (line 1297), and "Fen's gone" (line 1421), but she is alive on the Shelf at the time. "Gone" here means her core is destroyed, not her body. The file does not say what happens to a Shelf resident whose core breaks. Possibly the person is unchanged and merely cannot be put back; this is not stated. Do not resolve it in the game.
7. Line 379 says the fire is "three hundred feet below the engineer". The engineer is on the *Kestrel* in the mooring-yard, so this is a loose phrase for the buttress and the Shelf 300 feet below the wall. It conflicts with nothing else.
8. Brief says Morning Refusal, Standing Order Nine, Lead Brothers etc. The file has Lead Brothers only (lines 85, 199, 247). It has no Standing Order Nine, no "Morning Refusal", no magazines and no tower or battery counts.
9. Dates: line 13 says Memoryday 23rd to Nullday 24th. Line 473 says the Organum is Stasisday 28th. The cutter takes four days (24 to 28), so it left on the 24th. Line 1445 puts the Woods wait from Nullday 24th to Stasisday 28th. These agree.
10. Line 1333: Seraphina has been "on the Watch only five days" by 28 Ashfall, though she is on the *Equity* from the 24th. The five days run from her arrival around 23 Ashfall, so she joined the Watch a day before the cutter came. No other volume seen here, so not checked further.

## Answers for the three design questions

(a) The Quartermaster's stores and a night on the wall-walk watching the basket: the only things this file says about the stores are that they held a basket in 413 and a coil of new hemp rope, and that a supply cart brought the blanket from his sister. It says nothing of what else is packed or where the bread comes from. The basket goes down from the crane at the end of the buttress at the third hour, by the postern at the foot of Tower Nine, with a lead-coated old man, a lantern, a lid on the basket and a knot tied for each basket. A watcher on the wall would see the Lead Brothers standing along the bare ground beyond the buttress.
(b) A place on the lip where the Office's carts are tipped: this file gives no location, no tipping and no cart route. It gives only the wording "conveyed under escort to Null-Watch and disposed of at the lip", and "two hundred residuals in carts, sent east", about five weeks before 28 Ashfall.
(c) Redoubt Zero and the Edge: Redoubt Zero is not in this file. The Edge is 30 yards beyond the buttress end, with bare grey ground and Lead Brothers between, and it draws silent people off the end.
