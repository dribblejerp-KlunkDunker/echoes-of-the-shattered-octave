# B3P3 lore log: Book Three, Part Three (Ch 9-12), 1773 lines, all read

Whole file is LATER: Frostmonth 419 (2nd to 13th), except where a line looks back to earlier Ashfall or before. Day names seen: Nullday, Rootday, Hearthday, Massday, Stasisday, Dawnday, Driftday, Memoryday (looks like an eight-day week; see Contradictions). Month length is never given.

## The wall and the Watch
- B3P3:47 "the lip of the world three hundred feet above, at the black line of the wall and the towers on it." Lip is 300 ft over the Shelf; wall is black, towers stand on it. Seen Frostmonth 419.
- B3P3:917 "the chant began. *Rootday, Hearthday, Massday.* Forty batteries. Forty crews at the bellows." Forty batteries; crews chant day-names (the talking) at dawn. Frostmonth 419 (a standing habit, so also Harvestmonth).
- B3P3:1063 "The great flared brass mouth on its iron carriage under its lead hood, at the top of the tower above the end of the Shelf." Battery Nine: brass bell, iron carriage, lead hood, on Tower Nine, over the ship end of the Shelf. Standing set-up, ruined 6 Frostmonth.
- B3P3:1063 "It pointed out over the edge of the world at the not-colour, as it had pointed every dawn for six years." Batteries aim at the not-colour; the Refusal has been fired every dawn since 413.
- B3P3:1063 "Turning on its rail." The gun swings on a rail and can be tilted down into the Rift.
- B3P3:1075 "The Refusal is withdrawn from the sector of Batteries Eight, Nine and Ten." Those three batteries are the sector; General's intent that the Refusal be withdrawn (from Nullday). Frostmonth 419.
- B3P3:1087 "Every sound in the world at once. The rain and the wind and the sea and a crowd in a square, poured out of the great brass mouth." What the Refusal is: every sound poured out; it breaks a quiet. Heard by Lyra 6 Frostmonth.
- B3P3:1097 "The Watch's every-dawn *no*, turned down into the Rift." The Refusal is the Watch's every-dawn "no". Loud enough to press you down; lasts about a minute.
- B3P3:1317 "four miles of obsidian and lead and a garrison that cannot stop talking." Wall length is four miles; made of obsidian and lead; garrison must talk (Standing Order Nine, not named here). Frostmonth 419.
- B3P3:1289 "He did not know about the talking. Nobody had told him." The Justiciar Lisle walked to the edge because nobody told him about the talking. The talking is what keeps you from the edge.
- B3P3:1243 "on the wall above the postern at Tower Nine ... in the voiceless wind." Wall-walk over a postern at Tower Nine; the wind carries no sound.
- B3P3:1259 "It died in the wind three feet from his mouth." Voiceless wind: a laugh dies three feet from the mouth. Same wind on the plain (537, 1331).
- B3P3:1271 "the postern" and B3P3:1499 "the open ground before the gate." Gate with open ground before it; postern beside Tower Nine; Sable comes up by the rope to stand in it.
- B3P3:1341 "At the top of Tower Nine, three towers along." Gives Tower Nine as three towers from where Vane stands (conflicts with 1243; see Contradictions).
- B3P3:1353 "the stair inside the obsidian, round and round" and "Dust came down out of the joints in the black glass. The lead mortar cracked." Towers are hollow with a spiral stair, black glass joined with lead mortar.
- B3P3:1357 "The lead hood was gone ... The bellows were burst. The long brass cylinder behind the breech had split ... The crew of Battery Nine, twelve of them." Battery parts: hood, bell, bellows, brass cylinder behind breech; crew of twelve. Wrecked 6 Frostmonth dusk.
- B3P3:1341 siege fire came from "a low rise a mile from the wall" (written at 1337). The Justiciary guns shell Tower Nine from a mile out; two balls hit the hood, one a piece of it flung.
- B3P3:1397 "Nothing between the Shelf and the thing under the Maw but the next battery along, Battery Ten, and Eight beyond it." Only Batteries Ten and Eight remain for the Shelf sector after Nine is lost. 6 Frostmonth.
- B3P3:1719 "On Nullday ... the guns on Batteries Eight and Ten fired the Refusal out over the edge of the world as they had every dawn for six years." Eight and Ten fire at dawn; nobody on the plain fires. (Nullday = about 9th or 10th Frostmonth.)
- B3P3:1073 "She had stood on that wall for six years, listening for exactly this." The General has stood the wall since the Rift opened (413).
- B3P3:1385 "She had shouted her voice to rags on this wall on the first night of the Rift and it had gone over the edge and never come back." General Ward has no voice; first night of the Rift. Rift opened about summer 413 (inferred from six years and from 11).
- B3P3:577 "the black line of the Watch and the not-colour beyond, which you could not look at." The not-colour lies beyond the wall and cannot be looked at.
- B3P3:1287 "The General lifted her hand and struck her breastplate. A short phrase." Iron-cant: she speaks by striking her breastplate; a trooper translates (94, then Davey).
- B3P3:1321 "Then she struck her breastplate. Four blows. Flat, heavy, slow." Four blows mean "The Watch holds the wall" (1325).
- B3P3:1411 "The crews learn the battery calls and that's all. I learned the lot." Crews know only battery calls in iron-cant; Davey knows all of it.
- B3P3:1419 "*his name was Elric Orrel. Of Fenwick, in the Reach. Penal legion, Null-Watch, from 416, for debt. Adjutant to the General.* ... *Discharged.*" 94 is Elric Orrel; the Watch is a penal legion; posted for debt; "Discharged" is how a death is entered. Joined 416.
- B3P3:1425 "the battery-sergeant with the blackened face said, very loudly, '*Rootday.*' And they all said it with her." When a trooper dies the crew stops talking three breaths, then says the day-name. 6 Frostmonth.
- B3P3:1301 and 1331 the Dread Arbiter reads terms "under a white flag"; white flag "snapped in the voiceless wind with no sound at all." Parley is held at the gate on open ground.
- B3P3:1281 "the residuals at the lip, whom I am told you have been feeding, contrary to the writ, for six years." The Watch has fed the Shelf for six years against orders; the Office/Arbiterium knows. Frostmonth 419.
- B3P3:1285 "Thirty-two men of the Justiciary came to this garrison on the twenty-third of Ashfall to post the writ. Under a Justiciar. Corvan Lisle." A writ party arrives 23 Ashfall 419; none returned.
- B3P3:1289 "nineteen were killed on the buttress, in the fight, after they shot the Quartermaster in the back for carrying bread. Three on the cutter. Ten walked to the edge." Fate of the 32; the Quartermaster killed on the buttress carrying bread (after 23 Ashfall).
- B3P3:1303 "On Nullday, at the Refusal, the second writ falls. Batteries Eight, Nine and Ten will stand." The writ would silence the batteries and dispose of the residuals; later stayed (1693).
- B3P3:1693 "I'll speak to my son. About the writ ... Stay it. Not lift it." The writ is stayed, not lifted.

## The rope and the basket

## The Shelf
- B3P3:123 "She had sat by every one of them at the fire at one time or another, in six years, and talked." One fire; the Shelf is six years old as a place.
- B3P3:197 "the fire, and the holes in the wall, and the lean-tos, and the three hundred and sixty-five people coming out of them." Dwellings are holes in the wall and lean-tos; 365 people on Shelf at Frostmonth 2.

## How the Rendered come

- B3P3:163 "Her body was in the residual ward at the Malting. Five weeks ago they closed the ward. Two hundred residuals. Carts. The lip." The Malting ward was cleared about five weeks before 2 Frostmonth (late Harvestmonth or Ashfall 419; month length unknown). 200 residuals by cart.

- B3P3:1281 and 1303 the Arbiterium's own writ calls them "the residuals at the lip" and "disposed of, as routed." Disposal is by routing; the writ is an order to stop feeding them.
- Where on the lip the carts are tipped, who tips them, escorts, what is left on the lip: nothing in this file. Only that the lip is 300 ft over the Shelf and the Shelf is directly the landing.

## Contradictions
- Tower Nine: 1243 and 1471 put Vane "on the wall above the postern at Tower Nine", while 1341 says Tower Nine is "three towers along" from him.
- Days: 321 says "the writ fell on the third Nullday ... Seven days" from Nullday 2nd; header at 1237 says to Massday the thirteenth; 1719 puts Nullday after Driftday and Memoryday. Works only with an eight-day week (Dawnday between Stasisday and Driftday); the press does not run on Dawnday (Casimir's visit day, 1471).
- Shelf count: 365 (197) vs 364 testifiers excluding Orrin (223) vs 363 dockets (119-121) with Fen having no card; the 14 Hollows are not clearly inside any of these. "forty-seven" names also read at 143.
- Basket time: 1547 says 30 seconds to descend 300 ft; 1557 says "all the time it took a basket to go down three hundred feet." Interior sense: unknown, not a hard conflict.
- Bread cadence: "every night" (19) vs "morning basket" (299), "evening" (315), dusk (1747).
- Quartermaster: dead (1289, 1543) yet "the quartermaster says six by Rootday" (1371): so a new Quartermaster, or an office, still exists in Frostmonth.
- Hollows' count: 14 sit at the far end all through Ch 9-10 though Agathe Morrow and Davey are returned; 13 left at 1759.
