# BIBLES.md: the Continental Rift in the nine World Bible volumes (read-only recheck, 22 Nov 2026)

Short names: V6 = Vol VI, V2 = Vol II, V1 = Vol I, V3 = Vol III, V3b = Vol III (b), V4, V5, V7 = Vol VII (calendar), SB = Series Bible (canon). Vol VII (b) (festivals) and Vol VIII were not used for lore (VII(b) has nothing on the Rift). Line numbers are real (Read tool or grep -n).

Game date: Harvestmonth 419 (month 10 of 12, per V7:37). WHEN tags: BEFORE-419, HARVEST-419, LATER (Ashfall/Frostmonth 419 = the book's own act, never to be done or pre-empted by the player), or AFTER.

Precedence used: manuscript > V6 (marks overrides RULING) > V3b > V3 > V4/V5/V7 > V2 > V1. V3 strikes parts of V1 and V2. SB's Book Three chapter map (lines 417-440) is a summary of the manuscript and is treated as near-manuscript.
