# Lore reader's brief: the sky, the faiths, and old stones (22 Nov 2026)

You are a READ-ONLY lore reader for the browser RPG "Echoes of the Shattered Octave". The owner wrote the books in
`/home/claude/echoes-of-the-shattered-octave/story/`. Do not edit anything in that repo. Do not run a browser. Do not
spawn agents. Write ONE output file (path given in your task), then report in under 200 words.

Why: the owner has asked for constellations with lore, and "an added religion based around the galaxies, stars and
planets", with standing stones placed about the world that frame the constellations. Before a word of it is
invented, we must know everything the books and volumes already say, so the new faith contradicts nothing and
hooks onto what is there.

## Collect every passage about
- **The sky**: stars, the moon or moons (how many, names, phases), the sun, planets or "wanderers", comets, the
  night sky's look, the band of star-dust, the "Zodiac Drift" (a high sky-current: why is it called that?), any
  constellation or star named, anything navigated or reckoned by stars ("some reckoning of the stars, some Synod
  calculation"), eclipses, the sky's "wrongness" near any Spire, the Octave lights or aurora.
- **Faiths, churches and rites**: the Rite, the Synod, hymns and the Hymnal, the Dawning and the sun-altar, the Lead
  Brothers, the Hush as a faith, chaplains and sermons, shrines, saints, prayers, blessings, oaths, burial customs,
  what people swear by, heresies, any old or dead religion, any folk belief, what the Arbiterium permits or forbids.
- **Cosmology**: the Octave, the eight notes, the Gates and the Spires (each one's name, place and nature), how the
  world was made or broken ("the Shattered Octave"), what is above the sky, what the eight are.
- **The calendar**: month names and their meanings, the eight day-names and their meanings, feasts and their dates,
  the hours, the year-count's epoch (year 1 of what?), anything about seasons, solstices, harvest signs.
- **Old stones and old peoples**: standing stones, menhirs, cairns, barrows, stone circles, monoliths, ruins older
  than the present order, the people who raised them, shepherds' or sailors' or carters' lore, hill-figures,
  waymarks, boundary stones, anything "older than" the towns.
- **Numbers that recur**: eight, nine, twelve, any sacred number.

## How to write it
- Work grep-first with `grep -n -i`, then read each hit's whole passage with the Read tool. Line numbers must be real.
- For each passage: `short-name:line`, a SHORT exact quote (under 40 words) in quotation marks, one plain sentence on
  what it establishes, and WHEN it is (before 419, Harvestmonth 419, later).
- Group under the five headings above, then: **Contradictions** (where volumes disagree, and which rules: the
  manuscripts beat the bibles; Vol III and III(b) strike parts of Vol I and II), **Gaps** (what the books never say:
  for example whether any constellation is named), and **Hooks** (five to ten places where a faith of the stars could
  join what is already there without changing it).
- If a subject has nothing in your files, write "nothing". Do not invent. Do not summarise from memory.
