# Phases 52–55 — Release road R9 items 10–13: Tamsin Reed, Manifest, the slate and the bells, the Church comes up the road

*Recorded 7 Nov 2026 from the game's translation files (`tools/i18n/`), which hold every new player-facing line, in English with its French. Where a line is marked [PROPOSED] in the game or here, it is invention that has not been ruled on. Lines taken from the manuscript are the book's, cut to fit.*

## What is new, in short

- **Tamsin Reed and "Tell Mam" (R9 item 10)** (`js/tamsin52.js`). Book One ch. 5 and 7: the Legionnaire with the reed grille at Depot Three, three ways to break her bell by calling, the slag-slope, the Factor's "Did you see anything?", the clearing saying her name back at dawn; in Part Three, Sable's Ledger and her mother on the Bell Yard.
- **Manifest (R9 item 11)** (`js/manifest53.js`). Book One ch. 16 in the Kestrel's hold: the tags from memory, one thousand three hundred and forty-two, "Read you three", and "You are not lost. You are filed."
- **The slate and the bells (R9 item 12)** (`js/audit54.js`). A Legionnaire in earshot asks you to choose: say it aloud or write it on the slate. The Factor's morning walk hears back what the bells heard, and it costs you.
- **The Church comes up the road (R9 item 13)** (`js/church55.js`). Book One ch. 7 at sunrise on Dawnday: the gatehouse, the column, the warrant, Lyra taken into the Church's protection, the well-house; the Pure Note over the town until the Rite.
- **French that now shows on cards.** The translator works one text node at a time, so card text broken by `<br>` or `<i>` never matched its whole-string entry. `tools/i18n/merge-pairs.js` now also files each piece between tags; re-run over every older pair file (adding only missing pieces, changing nothing), it added 314 pieces, which brings the French of earlier scenes (the Rite, the Greening, Ash in the Hold, the forty-one nights, and others) onto their cards.

## Questions for sleeves

Everything below is [PROPOSED] and waits for your ruling.

**Tamsin Reed (R9 item 10)**
1. That the Factor asks *you* "Did you see anything?" (in the book she asks Kaelen), and that you can tell her the truth instead.
2. Sable's entry for Tamsin: number 40,270, nineteen, a dairymaid of the Bell Yard, taken on the eighth day, "will my mother be told?", and that Sable wrote it herself and said yes.
3. That the Hollow at the window on the Bell Yard (already in the game, setting out a blue jug and a white one) is Tamsin's mother; and the three endings there: set the white one aside, say her daughter's name, or leave her be.
4. Taking her by the bell costs the glassed 3 points of the tideline. Reading her name costs 6 voice.
5. The quest's name, "Tell Mam", from Tamsin's last words.

**Manifest (R9 item 11)**
6. It plays the first time you go down into the hold after Sable joins, rather than on the night she climbs the ladder. Corwin Aye's barrel-mark as a side beat is not built.

**The slate and the bells (R9 item 12)** — the whole mechanic is proposed
7. Which conversations ask: Kaelen, Lyra, Tobiah and Vane, in Part One, once per person per day, with a Legionnaire within seven paces and in sight.
8. What it costs: Vosk's store up 15% for each thing heard (45% at most); a second Legionnaire (Orrin Tey) at Depot Three after a fight or a body there; at the Rite her watching starts 2.2 seconds ahead for each thing heard (5.4 at most, of 18).
9. Writing on the slate costs ten minutes and nothing else.

**The Church comes up the road (R9 item 13)**
10. For the glassed calling only: the Inquisitor asks "Do you know what the Church calls men like you?", and you can answer "Consecrated", tell him it let go because they held ("Two of you. How very instructive."), or say nothing.
11. The Pure Note hums faintly in Oakhaven's streets from the Church's arrival to the Rite (the book: "It would not stop now until Hearthday"). This is part of R6's "Pure Note drone".
12. Not built: setting `rite_ready` on the canon day (the brainstorm's consequence); the Rite still waits for the coil.
