# Phase 69 — The Prologue, "The Cracked Bell", played as an extra (Book One, Prologue)

**Built 10 November 2026** (`js/bell69.js`; test `test/bell69.test.js`; ROADMAP R9 #23; the overnight plan's queue item 4). Every new line of in-game text, in English with its French. Status: **canon (book)** for every line of the scene: the Prologue of the full manuscript, ll. 8–226, quoted word for word in the cards and messages, cut only at paragraph breaks; **[PROPOSED]** for the play around them and for the buttons, hints, labels and HUD words.

**How it is reached.** Title screen, Extras, "The Cracked Bell". It runs in the extra's own session (EXTRAS17), so the Book One save is put aside untouched and no Book One flag is written. Its card and banner say "The Prologue of Book One" (an additive `tag` option in `js/extras17.js`) instead of "An extra — after Book One". Its unlock is the extras' own (Book One truly finished; the author's ruling FIX17 is unchanged).

**[PROPOSED] choices, for the owner:**
- **The player is Solenne Aubade.** The traveller does not exist in 412; the only way to take part without changing the book is to be the girl whose eyes it is told through. She does what the book has her do: listen, count, look, and say "Look at me" with her hands. Her mouth gives only the note (the Song key plays the book's paragraph on trying to speak, then only the note).
- **Thirty-nine voices with her own.** "She counted the voices around her in the dark. Thirty-nine. There were supposed to be forty." is read as counting her own, because the Swell has "Thirty-nine voices rose on the one pitch". The loft has forty pallets: thirty-eight girls asleep, Ilse's empty, her own empty.
- **The plan of the floor.** The book names the parts and how they stand to each other, not distances: the Spire's flank to the north with the seam and the Carrow Bore in it; the glass channels at its foot and down to the tanker bays and the conduit; the gantry; the risers with the top tier nearest the Spire ("threw her backward, off the top tier, over the glass channels, into ... the wound"); the censer platform the choir faces; the gallery above and behind it.
- **Her place** is the top tier, at Coralie's right hand ("Coralie ... had stood at Solenne's left shoulder").
- **Listening** (stand still and the chord comes closer) paces the Swell; moving only pauses it.
- **Looking up at the gallery** is the player turning to it; if they do not, it comes after fifteen seconds (in the book she looks).
- **The Echo.** No girl of the choir ever steps down off the risers in the book; walking off the top tier during the Swell or the light is an Echo (RITE43.echo), grey, and you are back in your place at the same moment.
- **Voices.** The engineer is unnamed in the Prologue and his line is cast as `kaelen_foundry` (the same voice as Kaelen's, before the glass arm: no glass ring). The Tuning Men are a new cast, `tuningman`. Ignatius is the existing `ignatius`. Thirteen lines are recorded; Solenne has no spoken line.
- **The HUD calendar** still shows Book One's calendar (a weekday and a day of Harvestmonth); the book gives no date in 412 beyond "the three hundredth sunrise". Not changed here.
