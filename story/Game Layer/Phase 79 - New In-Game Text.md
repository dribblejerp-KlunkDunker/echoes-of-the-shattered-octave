# Phase 79 pilot: the long road (the Greenroot road)

Owner's direction, 11 Nov: a world that takes a while to cross, and on every stretch something to find, someone to meet with a small task, and something that only happens at night or in bad weather. Of the books' two days' walk from Greenroot Hamlet to Oakhaven: "yes they did walk for days so keep that. We just have to be sure that there is stuff to do on that walk." And of the land: "please be sure to to not make the road between each area be a straight line and add lakes, streams, cliffs and on and on."

**Canon used:** the Greenroot road and the iron-bark oaks on either side of it (Book Two); the oaks ring in the wind (the game's own line for an oak); the two days' walk; the forty grain wagons that came up the Greenroot road in the spring of 413 (Book One ch. 2, and *What Came Up the Greenroot Road* in the Library), which is why the drover says no toll has been taken since; the Grey Teeth on the northern skyline. **Everything below is [PROPOSED]** for the owner's ruling: the toll-house and its board, the ford and its post, the plank bridge, the three milestones and what is cut on them, the road-mender, the drover and every line they speak; and every name on the land: **Oak Hill**, **the Shelf** (the scarp north of the road), **the Leap** (the fall the stream makes off it), **the Hang** and **the Little Hang** (two crags), **the Narrows** (the pass between them), the mere and its **eyot**, the tarn, the beck, and the cairn.

**Open point for the owner.** The Book Four prologue already in the game (Phase 17) walks the last of this road in the snow and passes milestones numbered II to V. The three stones here carry words, not numbers (the North Gate, HALF WAY, Greenroot Hamlet). Both can be true if this stretch lies beyond the fifth milestone; say if the stones here should carry numbers instead.
