# After Phase 87: Daggerfall Unity ideas to bring in, and in what order (for ruling)

The owner asked on 14 Nov whether anything in Daggerfall Unity could help this game, and then: "yes please can you please do that after this phase". This is that short list. These are **ideas only**. Each one would be written again in our own code, in our own way. No Daggerfall Unity code or assets are used.

What the game already has from that family: swings aimed by dragging the mouse, a far view on the roads, a travel map, day and night, weather. What it does not have: weapons that move with weight in the hand, a shield that shows the blows it takes, pack weight, or wear on gear.

## The list, in the order recommended

| # | Idea | What it would do here | Size | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Weapons with weight, and a shield that takes knocks** | Builds straight on Phase 87's new pictures. The weapon in your hand lags a little behind when you turn and settles when you stop, more for a maul than a knife. A blow you block jolts the shield. It keeps the marks it took (scratches, a dent, chipped paint on the dead queen) until you rest at an inn. The marks are only for show. | small | none to balance |
| 2 | **Swings that follow the blade's arc** | You already aim a swing by dragging the mouse. The picture would follow that line, overhead, side or thrust, with a faint smear behind the edge, so you see the stroke you made. | small to medium | none to balance |
| 3 | **Pack weight** | Everything you carry has a weight. Over your limit, you walk slower and tire faster. Capes and the heaviest armour weigh the most. Strength raises the limit. Pack animals (the pony from Phase 84) carry the rest. It would have its own Options switch, off by default for saves made before it. | medium | changes balance: the numbers would come to you first |
| 4 | **Wear and repair** | Weapons and armour wear with use and lose a little of their bite or protection. Smiths, the tanner and the Works mend them for coin. A worn set still counts as a set. | medium to large | changes balance and shops: the numbers would come to you first |
| 5 | **More of the far land** | More of the land drawn far off on the main roads (the hills, the woods' edge, the lights of a town at night), in the game's own pixel look. | large | performance on phones: it would keep a Low setting |

**Recommendation:** 1 and 2 first, together, as one phase (Phase 88). They only make the gear just built feel better, and they change nothing a save depends on. 3 and 4 after that, as one phase, with the numbers for your ruling first. 5 last, and only if the road work does not already cover it.

## For your ruling

1. **Bring in 1 and 2 next, as Phase 88?** *Recommend: yes.*
2. **Pack weight (3) and wear (4): wanted at all?** Both make the game harder and slower. Some players love them and some do not, which is why each would have its own switch. *Recommend: yes, as switches, numbers to you first.*
3. **The far land (5): later, or not at all?** *Recommend: later, after the roads.*

Every line above is [PROPOSED].
