# Phases 50–51 — Release road R9 items 7–8: Ash in the Hold and the forty-one nights

*Recorded 7 Nov 2026 from the game's translation files (`tools/i18n/`), which hold every new player-facing line, in English with its French. Where a line is marked [PROPOSED] in the game or here, it is invention that has not been ruled on.*

## What is new, in short

- **Ash in the Hold, played (R9 item 8)** (`js/ash50.js`). Book One ch. 13 as one playable night aboard the Kestrel, offered by the Part Two ending's button (or slept through). The field hospital, Hetty's count, the slate, the Root through the glass, Vane in the hold, Hob at the fourth hour, Tobiah at first light, the calling-song and the beam keep the book's lines closely. Hob dies as in the book, as the old Part Three card already had it; the Kestrel's crew bunk stays Abner Fenning's.
- **The forty-one nights (R9 item 7)** (`js/lyra51.js`). Book One ch. 1 and 3: the stone behind the old cooperage, Lyra on it at the dead hour of the forty-first night (the night after the Madder birth, by the book calendar the game now keeps), and the forty-second night, when the Hedge hums Kaelen's note to the empty stone.

## Questions for sleeves

Everything below is [PROPOSED] and waits for your ruling.

**Ash in the Hold, played (R9 item 8)**
1. The seams before dawn as play: five green seams to prise up with Pennick in 140 seconds; a missed one leaves a pale new plank in the Kestrel's hold in Part Three (the book's Pennick finds none).
2. You bring the water, the tonic or the tending note to the wounded, and you carry the slate to Lyra (in the book Kaelen does).
3. Tobiah's accusation aimed at you, since the game has you seat the coil; telling him why or saying nothing. If he still has his charts, he gives them to you here, as in the book.
4. Kaelen wearing the coat from the aired room at the beam, if you left it on the hut's step, or putting it on when you give it to him.
5. The children counted as nine, the number the rope scene already uses.

**The forty-one nights (R9 item 7)**
6. "Tell Kaelen in the morning": he says he will go to her door that night, and the forty-second night's card says she heard his note at the latch because you told him (lines only).
7. "Answer it yourself": it learns your voice (the game's existing hedge_answered), and Lyra goes past you without a word.
8. Not built, waiting for your ruling: the Brainstorm's fourth way, sitting beside her so that it will not speak and she never goes back. It breaks from the book (no forty-first night, no meadow as written).
