# Phase 75 — Small Canon Touches III: New In-Game Text

*Recorded 11 Nov 2026 from the game's translation file (`tools/i18n/p75-touches.tsv`). ROADMAP R9 #25, the third batch of the Story Brainstorm's twenty small canon touches (c, d, e, f), in `js/touches75.js`. These four were held back from the first two batches because each needs a small design decision. The owner's ruling (10 Nov): "keep everything book accurate. If the proposed might help or add something to the game go for it." Every line is the manuscript's, put into the present tense and the second person where the game reads that way; the decisions and the few connecting words the book does not give are marked [PROPOSED]. Nothing is voiced: none of these lines is spoken by a book character.*

## What is new, in short

- **c. The Pure Note as a lid** (Book One ch. 9, l. 2850). *"By the second day nobody was sleeping. By the third, people had begun to plaster their windows with wet cloth and stuff wool in their ears, and children were crying in the lanes for no reason they could name… It was not a loud sound. It was worse than loud. It was a lid."* `js/church55.js` already holds the note over Oakhaven from the Church's coming until the Rite. Now, in the town, on the note's second day you are told that nobody is sleeping; from the third, the wet cloth, the wool and the crying children. Each is said once a game day. And a rest in Oakhaven or any of its rooms while the note holds is shorter: "You lie awake under it… It is a lid." The rest's own "eight hours" / "four hours" line (and the dark's "You sleep deeply, and snore") is not shown, since it is no longer true. The bed in your own house on Cooper's Row (`js/upgrades.js`) is shortened the same way.
  - [PROPOSED] Half the hours and half of what the rest gives back (health, voice, fatigue, and sleep and hunger when the journey needs are on), the way `js/night13.js` shortens a rest broken by a night terror. A lamp rest is four hours, a half rest two.
  - [PROPOSED] From the first night of the note, not only the second day; "in Oakhaven" is the town and its rooms (`LAW_MAPS`); never outside the Hedge, and not once the Rite has begun, the Greening runs or you have left on the Kestrel.
  - [PROPOSED] The days are counted from the day the note began (`p75_lid`, recorded by the game's tick on the day the note is first held, wherever you are; an old save already past Dawnday counts from Dawnday). The note's first day is `js/church55.js`'s scene and gets no line here.
  - Quest timing: a shorter rest only passes less time, so it cannot carry you past any day or hour a quest waits for. No existing test rests under the note.
- **d. Every mending borrows** (ch. 1, ll. 396–402; ch. 3, l. 1089). *"Out of the crease of her palm, where the life-line crossed the heart-line, grew a single root-hair. White. A thumbnail long… and the blood was very slightly green. Every mending borrows."* After a large tending note (sung, or hummed into the clay jar), a Cantor-calling player finds the root-hair, pulls it, and the blood is very slightly green. A message only.
  - [PROPOSED] "Large" is ten health or more mended by the one note (the sung note mends 14, the jar 20; a note sung at nearly full health does not count). At most once a game day (`p75_hair`). Only the Cantor calling: the book gives it to a Cantor.
- **e. The palm held flat** (ch. 1, l. 258). *"…to lift his hand, palm flat, the way everyone in Oakhaven measured sound now (a hand's width of voice, no more)…"* Inside the Hedge, when you are loud but not yet over the breach, a townsperson who can see you lifts a hand toward you, palm flat. At the breach `js/narrow28.js`'s ward-sign comes as before.
  - [PROPOSED] The band is noise 5 (the HUD's LOUD) up to narrow28's breach (6.5). The watcher is narrow28's own witness (in sight, facing you, not a companion); a named townsperson is named. Not in the Boar (narrow28 leaves the Boar its own rules), not in custody or while resisting the watch (narrow28's guard), not while anything is hunting you, and at most once a minute of play. Book characters only lift a hand; nobody speaks.
- **f. The Cantor's clay jar** (ch. 1, ll. 248–250; ch. 6, l. 1937). *"…a Cantor's trick from the orchard days, for when you wanted a sound to go into a body instead of into the air. The jar caught the note and gave it to the skin. Hummed into the open room, the tending note would have been a sound. Hummed into the jar, it was a warmth, a pressure, a hand laid flat where no hand could reach."* A Cantor-calling player carries a clay jar. Used from the pack, you press it to your own throat (as Lyra tests herself on Massday, l. 1937) and hum the tending note into it. It is the tending song itself, so every rule on songs still holds (a Quiet's dead air, a silence zone, a torn voice), but quieter, and it mends more.
  - [PROPOSED] The numbers, against the sung note (8 voice, noise 1.8, mends 14): the jar costs the same 8 voice, adds noise 0.9 (the murmur's) and mends 20.
  - [PROPOSED] The balance: not while anything is hunting you (an enemy giving chase within ten cells). The jar wants a still body, so it is a between-fights mending; in a fight you still sing.
  - [PROPOSED] Given once to every Cantor, new games and old saves alike (`p75_jar`). It is a key item: it cannot be sold, and the watch-house does not take it. Without the tending note it does nothing; straight after another song or a blow it says "Not yet."

## [PROPOSED] choices

1. c: a rest in Oakhaven or its rooms while the Pure Note holds is half as long and gives back half; from the note's first night.
2. c: the town's lines are said once a game day in the town, on the note's second day (nobody sleeping) and from its third (wet cloth, wool, crying children).
3. d: ten health or more from one tending note is "large"; once a game day; Cantor calling only.
4. e: noise 5 to 6.5, narrow28's witness, not in the Boar, once a minute.
5. f: the jar's numbers (8 voice, noise 0.9, mends 20), not while hunted, given once to every Cantor as a key item.
6. f: the item's name and the mechanics sentence in its description ("Hum the tending note into it (8 voice)…") are the game's.
