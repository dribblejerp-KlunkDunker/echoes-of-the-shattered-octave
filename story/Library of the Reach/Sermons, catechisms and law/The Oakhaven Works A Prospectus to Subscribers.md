# The Oakhaven Works: A Prospectus to Subscribers

*the Consortium of Bores, by order of the Seven Chairs (Lorimer Stannard, Secretary to the Subscription) · 417 SR · the Consortium's counting-house, Brass Quarter, the Organum · politics*

> The Consortium's invitation to invest in the new works at Oakhaven, generous with confidence and sparing with figures.

THE CONSORTIUM OF BORES. The Oakhaven Works, in the Resonant Reach. A Prospectus for the Second Subscription, issued by order of the Seven Chairs and approved under the hand of the First.

To the subscriber. The Consortium has the honour to invite your part in what the Chairs do not hesitate to call the most promising undertaking of the age.

THE SEAM. The Oakhaven Root lies beneath the old quarry of that town. Our surveyors, who are not given to poetry, describe it as the most generous resonant body yet found on the continent. It is alive in the agricultural sense: it grows, it renews itself, it gives. Where a seam of stone is spent, a living seam refreshes. We do not expect the subscriber to take this on trust, and so we have not burdened this prospectus with figures, which in the case of a seam of this character could only understate it.

THE METHOD. The works are sunk by the Vance Sequence, the Consortium's own method, used on every great bore since 409 and lately much improved by the finest mathematical minds in the Organum. Revision follows revision. The subscriber may rest assured that the Root will be approached with all the care that the most advanced resonant engineering of the age can bring to it.

THE TOWN. Oakhaven is a loyal and grateful town. It was the Consortium's grain, forty wagons of it, that brought the Reach through the famine after the Screaming Year, and the Reach has not forgotten. The Governor is a man of sense. The labour is local, willing, strong in the arm and used to hard country. It is paid in Consortium scrip, redeemable at the works store, which keeps the subscriber's money in the subscriber's own house.

Supplementary labour for the pumps is supplied under contract by the Office of the Catalogue. It asks no wage, no lodging and no rest, and gives no trouble of any kind.

SECURITY. The works enjoy the protection of a detachment of the Carillon Legions. Replacement components for the garrison are delivered by air on a regular schedule, at no charge to the subscription.

OF THE SO-CALLED RISKS. The subscriber may have read, in the less responsible papers of the Rust Coast, that the Reach is 'going soft'. We answer that the Reach has always been soft. It is orchard country. Indeed the vigour of the seam already shows itself, most agreeably, in the town above it. Saplings come up between the cobbles in a night. Hair and nails grow at a rate the townsfolk find remarkable, and the barbers of Oakhaven have never been busier. A local complaint called burl-sickness, long known in the province and wholly unconnected with the works, is attended by the Synod's Surgeon-Grafters, to whose good work the Consortium contributes generously.

It has been put about that the hounds are bolder in the Reach of late. The Consortium keeps no hounds and cannot speak for them. The town has in hand a scheme of hedging, of approved local design, which will make Oakhaven the quietest town in the Reach and, the Chairs believe, the most productive.

It has also been asked whether the Church takes an interest in the works. The Church takes an interest in everything, and the Consortium welcomes it. Where the Church and the Consortium are at one, nothing in the province can stand against them, and they are at one on every point that touches a subscriber.

RETURNS. A dividend will be declared on the Dial Day following the casing, payable in sovereigns at the Organum or, at the subscriber's choice, in scrip at the works. The Chairs do not think it proper to name a figure. They will say only that no subscriber to the Consortium's great works at the Dawning Spire has ever once been asked to return a dividend.

HOW TO SUBSCRIBE. Shares of ten sovereigns, in lots of ten, are received at the Consortium's counting-house in the Brass Quarter, or by the Resident Factor at the Oakhaven works, whose receipt is stamped with the drill-and-wheel. Subscriptions close when the bore reaches the casing. The Chairs anticipate that this will be soon.

The Seven Chairs commend the Oakhaven Works to you in the confident expectation that it will repay everything that is put into it. What the ground gives, the Consortium gathers. The Root is generous. It has never yet refused anyone anything.

Lorimer Stannard, Secretary to the Subscription. Firstfruit, 417.
