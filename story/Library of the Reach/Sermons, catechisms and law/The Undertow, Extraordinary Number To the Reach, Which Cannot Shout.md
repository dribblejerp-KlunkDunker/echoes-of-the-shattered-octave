# The Undertow, Extraordinary Number: To the Reach, Which Cannot Shout

*the Undertow, Broadsheet Row, Port Cantabile (unsigned) · 419 SR · Broadsheet Row, Port Cantabile; carried up the river through Stillwater Landing · pamphlet*

> A seditious Cantabile broadsheet printed small to fold small, written for the whispering towns of the Reach and smuggled in by the river.

THE UNDERTOW. Broadsheet Row, Port Cantabile. Extraordinary Number, printed small to fold small. Price one penny to the lockman at Stillwater, who will charge you two. Not for sale in the Reach, where nothing is for sale that the works store does not sell.

TO THE REACH, WHICH CANNOT SHOUT, FROM A STREET THAT CANNOT STOP.

Friends up the river. We are told you read in a whisper now. Good. Whisper this.

You were hungry after the Screaming Year, and a company came up your road with forty wagons of grain and a paper. You ate the grain. Nobody blames you. We would have eaten it. But you might ask, the next time you stand at the works store paying for bread with the green-and-black paper they pay your sons in, who owns the store, and who owns the paper, and who owns the bread, and whether there is anything left in Oakhaven that a man is paid in and can spend anywhere else.

They took your bells. Every bell in the Reach, for 'the public works'. Ask what a bell is for at the bottom of a bore. We asked an engineer. He drank what we bought him and said 'teeth', and would say no more, and was gone before the second glass.

They gave you a Hedge instead. Thorn and wool, three men deep, to swallow your sounds. You planted it yourselves in a night, and we hear it was the bravest piece of work done in that province in a hundred years. Now ask which side of it the works are on, and which side of it you are.

THE BELL-HELMS. There is a garrison of Carillon Legionnaires at your quarry gate. Stand close to one, if you can bear to, and listen. Every soldier who has served beside them has heard it: somebody is murmuring inside the helm. We do not say whose voice. We do not know. We say only that crates come up this coast marked 'components', very carefully packed, and go inland by air; and that a man who loaded them for two seasons at Driftwood Cay told us in the Lantern Yard that they talk in their sleep. Then he stopped coming to the Lantern Yard. His wife does not know where he is either.

WHISPER-GROVE. Three years ago a city of seventy-four thousand forgot itself in a night, and the Synod's Gazette called it a regrettable resonance event and put a cordon round it. Our neighbour on the Row, whom we do not like and who does not like us, printed what his men saw inside the cordon. We have checked it. He was right. We say so through our teeth.

THE CHURCH. Its processions are out on the roads this year under glass-fire, with warrants in boxes. They sing the whole way, aloud, in daylight, and the hounds pace them along the tree-line and do not come in. You will want very badly to believe that this means something. It means that they have the one fire the hounds will not cross, and they have never once offered to share it with you.

WHAT WE ASK. Nothing. We are a newspaper. The Harbour lets the Row print because the Row has never asked anybody to do anything, and we are not fools enough to start. We only say that we hear things go missing in the Reach: a cart, a carter, a whole parish on the Flume road where nobody answers letters now. Write to us. The lockman at Stillwater will carry a letter down the river for a penny. He will read it first and sell what is in it to five other people, so write it plain, and write it as if all five were reading, because they are.

HOW TO KEEP THIS. Do not burn it. Smoke carries, and the Flambeaux have noses. Do not read it aloud: they say the bell-helms keep everything they hear and can be made to say it all back. Fold it in eight and put it in a hymnal in your chapel, at the page for the Pure Note, where nobody ever needs to look. Somebody will find it. That is how it found you.

A WORD FROM THE ROW. Our compositor of the last number is not with us. He was careful and he was quick, and he set this paper for nine years without a literal in it. We have set this one ourselves, and you will find the mistakes. Leave them. They are how you know it was us.
