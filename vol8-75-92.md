# World Bible Vol VIII, The Game Layer: Phases 75 to 92

The game-lore entries added to Vol VIII (`story/World Bibles/Vol VIII - The Game Layer.md`, Part IV) on 15 Nov 2026, on the owner's rule: "every update needs to be added to the game lore." The full volume is in the repository; this extract is the new part.

#### Phases 75 to 78 and R11: how the world looks (11 to 12 Nov; `js/pixelfolk75.js`, `js/paintfolk76.js`, `js/walls77.js`, `js/eaves78.js`, the R11 renderer)
These phases changed how the world is drawn, not what is in it. Phase 75 drew four people (Kaelen, Lyra, an Oakhaven orchardman, a Carillon Legionnaire) from a skeleton as moving pixel figures. Phase 76 kept the paintings and made every NPC's painting move: walk, idle, talk, hurt and strike, from eight sides. Orla (seated), the cat and the seated map figure stay still on purpose. Phase 77 sharpened every wall, roof and ground tile in the 3D view. Phase 78 gave the buildings of Oakhaven, the Hollow, Greenroot, Port Cantabile and Whisper-Grove the shapes of houses (pitched roofs, eaves, fronts) in the 3D view. No lore changed. **Status:** design, no canon claims.

#### Phases 79 and 80: the Greenroot road and Singers' Howe (12 to 13 Nov; `js/road79.js`, `js/road79b.js`, `js/howe79.js`)
The book's two days' walk from Greenroot Hamlet to Oakhaven became a road of three stretches, about 900 steps:
- **The Long Common** is dale and moor country: the Delph, an old stone pit with glass grown up through its rock; a clapper bridge; a wayside well with a hush-lamp; Crow Tor; Long Water; the Saddleback; the Ringing Stand and the Bell Oak.
- **The middle stretch** has Oak Hill, the toll-house, the mere and its eyot, the Narrows between the Hang and the Little Hang, and the Shelf with its fall, the Leap.
- **Greenroot Wood** climbs to the reed-row and the lych-house.

The iron-bark oaks on either side ring in the wind, and are silent in snow (canon). Travellers and bands of explorers walk the road. Its milestones are worded, not numbered. Off the road lies **Singers' Howe**, a barrow-dungeon whose keeper is **the Long Rest**: a Quiet grown strong, named for the silence written into music. **Status:** canon (ruled). The owner approved every Phase 79 proposal on 13 Nov ("i approve of all proposals") and the stretch names on 12 Nov. The Long Rest was [PROPOSED] in Phase 80.

#### Phase 81: the world plan (13 Nov; `story/Game Layer/Phase 81 - The World Plan.md`)
There are three countries, each walked inside itself, with the Kestrel flying between them. The books have no walking road from the Reach to the Woods or to the Coast, so none is built. Six roads and four big places were planned in the Reach, the Woods and the Coast. Each road is drawn from a real country and has one wonder that no real country has. The plan proposes that the Hedge was planted along the foot of the Ladder: the bible puts the Low Rings inside Oakhaven, and the game reaches them from outside. It also turned up a canon detail for later: the boy at the fountain in the Plaza of the Willows, whose loop in Harvestmonth is "four, and then we go" (Book Four). **Status:** canon (ruled), approved 13 Nov ("approve all"). Still the owner's to give: the four words of the woman in the doorway.

#### Phases 82 and 82b: the regions' buildings, the map, finger-posts and notice boards (13 Nov; `js/eaves82.js`, `js/bearings82b.js`, `js/boards82b.js`)
Six more building styles:
- **Stillwater Landing:** black-tarred board under reed thatch; every window looks at the water.
- **The Low Rings:** a tide-line on every wall, and one lit loft.
- **Wethercombe:** flint under deep thatch, and a straw ram on the Bellwether's ridge.
- **The Watch Downs:** nothing roofed.
- **Driftwood Cay:** marram thatch roped down with stones.
- **The Flume Road:** living hoops over the water with a bead of sap-light at each crown after dusk.

Every outdoor place now sits on its region's map where its roads say it does. Finger-posts stand at every way out and every fork. Every town has a notice board with notices, lore and side quests. Greenroot's board carries its last notices, weathered. **Status:** 82b canon (ruled), 13 Nov ("I agree with all recommendations"). Phase 82's three questions (the Cay under thatch, the one lit window in the Low Rings, thatch on Wethercombe's fold walls) are still open.

#### Phase 83: the Flume road and the drove road (13 Nov; `js/flume83.js`, `js/drove83.js`)
**The Stride** runs through Sallow Bottom, a wet valley under the Flume, which crosses it on grown trunks as tall as a church. Sap-light runs green in the channel after dusk. The valley also holds Ennis Crowle's bee-boles (canon: Vol V's beekeeper) and a gauging stone. **The Pound Stair** has sixteen grown locks. The lengthsman's three stuck gates open to the Root sung in order. A raft has been wedged under Compact chalk for two years. Along the road are a broadsheet-runner, a Synod gauger and two raft-hands.

On the drove road, **the Grey Wethers** is a flock of ringing sarsens. Five stones are raddled for the drovers' call, three, one, four, two, five, which the old woman of the returning family hums. There are a dew-pond, the drovers' halt at **the Three Pines**, a pinfold and its tally-stick, and a furze-cutter cutting faggots for the beacons. **The Hundred Hollows** is a holloway under a hawthorn roof and a down of grassed pits. Hounds den there after dark, and mist fills the combe after a wet night. **Status:** canon (ruled), 13 and 14 Nov ("all proposed pass"; "I approve").

#### Phase 84: mounts and the long journey (14 Nov; `js/mounts84.js`)
Three stables keep three ponies each, in real coats: roan, bay and black; dun, chestnut and palomino; dapple grey, skewbald and leopard-spotted. A pony will not go onto planks, down the Pound Stair, into the Deep Grove or indoors. Hounds at night frighten it home, and it costs 5 sovereigns to fetch. Milestones stand by every finger-post. The world map travels along the roads with night halts, and ambushes come by stretch. **Status:** "the roan mare", "the dun" and "the grey" are canon (ruled); the rest is [PROPOSED].

#### Phase 85: the four big places of the Reach (14 Nov; `js/dryflume85.js`, `js/antler85.js`, `js/lanes85.js`, `js/grove85.js`)
- **The Dry Flume** is the stone conduit that ran before the Flume was grown. Its root hangs lit through the roof. It holds settling tanks, a stopped iron wheel, a sump that floods to the roof whenever the Flume above "draws a breath", and the pump-hall. Its keeper is **the Standing Note**, a mire-drone two hundred years grown, whose one note is the note the engine made. Its gear is the Lengthsman's Hook and the Sluice-Boots.
- **The Antler Galleries** are white chalk galleries where the antler picks left behind took root. They are siphoned, so the Hush comes. Tallow-cups stand in the niches. Below is the white forest of antler, the floorstone seam, and the keeper **the Flint-Back**, a fret-crawler grown white. Its gear is the Antler Pick and the Floorstone Knife.
- **The Sunk Lanes and Cross Pool:** the punt-woman and her punt. The drowned market cross is lit from beneath after dark. There are the weavers' garrets with their tuned reeds, and the Ladder's Foot, where you hear Oakhaven over the Hedge. On still nights the kerb house is reached by the old kerb-tops. The Punt-Pole and the Market-Warden's Buckler are found here.
- **The Deep Grove** lies through the gap in Greenroot Wood. The lower note leads in and any other leads out. The Stool sounds "not in any key the Octave uses". There are carters' marks dated the Reach way, by what was growing (Vol VII), the Carter's Oilskin and the Fallen Iron-Bough.

**Status:** [PROPOSED] under the approved plan (Phase 81's big places).

#### Phase 86: danger and reward on the roads (14 Nov; `js/roads86.js`)
What is out on a road depends on the hour and the weather.
- **Hostile camps:** an Office survey camp by the toll-house; a **Quiet Faith vigil** at the Grey Wethers, where three tongueless sit round a fire laid and never lit; a hound den; a briar-bed.
- **Friendly fires:** the raft-hands' fire and the fire at the Three Pines.
- **Four keepers, each with its own hour:** **the Hiss** (a hound the size of a pony, in rain at night); **the Briar Queen**; **the Hollow Dog** (in mist); **the Sunk Bell** (a drone grown under a fallen bell-tower, in storm).
- **Bounties** from the turf-cutter, the lengthsman, the returning father and the salvagers.
- **The Office column** (the Assessor, two proctors, an outrider and a hushwarden) walks one road a day.
- Every board carries "On the roads today".

**Status:** [PROPOSED] under the approved design. The Quiet Faith and the Office are canon (book).

#### Phase 87: gear, matching sets and the first-person view (14 Nov; `js/gear87.js`, `js/items87.js`, `js/fp87.js`)
There is now a cape slot, and fourteen matching sets dress the world's factions and trades, among them the Synod Registry, the Synod Arsenal, the Carillon Legion, the Cantors, the Siphon Corps, the Consortium Works, the Free Captains, the Drovers, the Barrow-Wardens, the Flume and the Lanes, and the Old Reach. Every calling starts in its own set. Fifty-two new pieces and six ranged weapons were added, each with its own look. **Status:** canon (ruled), 14 Nov. The owner's later rulings on the gambeson, the starting kits, the set bonuses, the throwing knives and the lighter quarry sledge are in the Phase 87 record.

#### Phase 88: the rules and the feel (14 Nov; `js/rules88.js`, `js/feel88.js`)
Blocking costs fatigue, and a winded fighter has no guard. Falls hurt. Pursuers follow you through doors, though bosses and keepers do not. Weapons swing with their weight. **Status:** canon (ruled), 14 Nov. The Arsenal's kite block, the Captains' ship's height and the Barrow-Wardens' door went back to their first wording.

#### Phase 89: the Continent (14 Nov; `story/Game Layer/Phase 89 - The Continent (lore and great cities, for ruling).md`)
This is the lore of all eight realms and the Organum's middle country, from the books and bibles with every source named:
- the Resonant Reach (Oakhaven);
- the Dawning Heights (Sol-Luminary);
- Forge-Spire Cinderhold;
- the Iron Peaks (Bastion Fortis);
- the Shifting Dunes (Sandglass Bazaar);
- the Tempest Archipelago and the Rust Coast (Port Cantabile);
- the Spectral Woods (Whisper-Grove);
- the Continental Rift (Null-Watch).

Each realm's great city is laid out on its own note's cymatic figure. The Souls frame gives four great places to a realm with a named keeper each (32 new), lamps, shortcuts and the Piece. There are about 70 smaller places, and a painted map of the continent. The ruling of this phase: **places are shown as of Harvestmonth 419, and what the books do later is never done by the player.** **Status:** canon (ruled), 14 Nov ("i agree with your recommendation about the new countries and all proposals pass"). Everything thin in the books that the record fills in stays marked [PROPOSED] there; that record is the gazetteer for these realms until each is built.

#### Phase 91: the Souls rules (14 to 15 Nov; `js/souls91.js`)
Five hush-lamps on iron posts stand in the Reach's great places. Resting at one heals, saves and brings the place's foes back; its keeper returns once a week (eight days) has passed. Die in a place with a lit lamp and you wake by the last lamp you lit. **The Piece** (your carried coin and progress toward the next level) waits where you fell, humming **its realm's note: 174 Hz in the Reach**. Die again before you reach it and it is gone. **The hour-bead** undoes a death; the hour-merchants who sell it are Veridia's, built with the Dunes. There are two shortcuts: the chimney from the Flint-Back's den to the shaft's foot, and the barred iron door into the pump-hall. **Status:** canon (ruled), 15 Nov:
- a Piece rings its realm's note;
- resting passes one hour;
- waking stays inside the place.

The fourth question, trophies and realm gear for the Reach's two keepers, comes back to the owner once the Heights' four keepers are built.

#### Phase 92: the Dawning Heights, part one: the Umbral Tiers (15 Nov; `js/heights92.js`, `js/heights92_grid.js`)
The Kestrel's map table flies south to Sol-Luminary in 30 hours, once Book One is done and the new number-three tank is fitted. The Umbral Tiers are one map cut from the city's 528 Hz figure. It runs from the Keel Docks up through the hanging houses, chain-walks and ladders, and past the cobbler's of the fourth tier, the black market under black canvas, the hanging square and the bell-gallery stair. It ends at the iron door in the Basilica's black wall, which is seen and not entered (the Basilica stands on the ninth tier, where the city stops hanging and begins to stand: canon).

**From the book (canon, Book Two ch. 9), matched 15 Nov:**
- The Keel Docks are **eleven iron cradles** hung on chains below the lowest tier over two thousand feet of air. The Kestrel lies in the fourth from the end, with a Church packet (the white sun-disc on its envelope) and two battered freighters in three of the others.
- The harbourmaster comes down veiled: a black hat and veil, black gloves, and a black lantern shuttered to a slit. Two pole-men with iron hooks walk in her shadow. She takes every mirror and lens that shows a face into a black velvet bag: "A reflection is a second light. The Light is jealous. They'll be returned when you leave the chains." The game gives your mirror back when you climb the Kestrel's ladder.
- **Brenna Corliss**, the Free Captains' woman in the Keel Docks, keeps a chandlery in a hanging house on the second tier under a sign that says ROPE, painted black on black. The right half of her face has been white since the morning of the Fracture: "Most people didn't have time to turn their heads." A letter from Oona Salt gets you through her door. The Tiers sell black cloth by the bolt, and she sells it.
- Everyone on the Tiers speaks in the Narrow.

**Invented, and the owner's:**
- The harbourmaster is named **Mother Ysolde Carrow**.
- **Anselm Dray**, the cobbler of the fourth tier, makes chain-boots soled with tarred rope.
- **The woman under the black canvas** sells the shade-cloak, the cloth-pole and hand-mirrors.
- **Joss Feveril** works on the seventh chain.
- The dangers are the Bleach below the line, the chain-walks' jerk on the chains' beat, the gulf, and bleach-hounds. The keeper is **the Lamplighter of the Seventh Chain**, who throws glass-fire and drops his lamp-hood and amber glass.
- Three shade-lamps stand on the Tiers, and a Piece here rings at **528 Hz**. The fifteenth gear set is the Umbral Tiers.

**Status:** canon (ruled), 15 Nov: the 30-hour flight, the new names ("the new names are good") and the customs. "Anselm Dray" shares a first name with Anselm Rooke; it was approved with the other names.
